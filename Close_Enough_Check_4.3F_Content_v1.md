# The Close Enough Check — Content v1 (MA.4.3F-XP)

**Grade 4 · Math · Frostveil.** Written Sept 26, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/close-enough-check.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.3F · **Also shows up under:** 4.3G

**I can:** I can use benchmark fractions like 0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, and 1 to estimate a sum or difference and tell if an answer makes sense. I can show fractions as distances on a number line.

**Mission:** Check the crew's fuel math with benchmark fractions and get everyone home before the blizzard.

**Story:** A blizzard is racing toward the fuel depot on Frostveil. Kai is doing the fuel math super fast, and some of his answers can't be right. Estimate with benchmark fractions to catch the mistakes before the storm hits.

**Meters on Frostveil:** Storm ready (starts at 35%) · Fuel cans · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {name}, this is Commander Vega. A big blizzard is heading for the fuel depot on Frostveil. Kai is doing the fuel math as fast as he can, but fast isn't always right. Use benchmark fractions to check if each answer is close enough to make sense. Vega out.
