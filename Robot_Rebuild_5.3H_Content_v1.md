# The Robot Rebuild — Content v1 (MA.5.3H-XP)

**Grade 5 · Math · Mechara.** Written Sept 26, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/`.

**Main standard:** 5.3H · **Also shows up under:** 5.3K, 5.3A

**I can:** I can add and subtract fractions with different bottom numbers by making equal-size pieces first. I can estimate to check if my answer makes sense.

**Mission:** Add and subtract fractions to recharge the robots and restart the city core.

**Story:** Mechara's robot city lost power, and its robots are stuck mid-job. Every battery, tank, and track in the city uses a different fraction.

**Meters on Mechara:** Power (starts at 30%) · Parts · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** Act 1 works with the task types already on the site. Acts 2–3 are written and graded but stay locked until the new task types are built.

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {name}, this is Commander Vega. Mechara's robot city just lost power. The robots froze in the middle of their jobs, and every battery and tank there is measured with a different fraction. Help Kai and Nova get the robots running again. Vega out.
