# The Cargo Bays — Content v1 (MA.4.4D-XP)

**Grade 4 · Math · Cindara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/cargo-bays.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.4D · **Also shows up under:** 4.4B, 4.4C

**I can:** I can multiply up to a four-digit number by a one-digit number and a two-digit number by a two-digit number, using mental math, partial products, area models, properties, and the standard algorithm.

**Mission:** Count the crates, fill every cargo bay, and launch the lava freighters before the volcano erupts.

**Story:** Loading day has come to the Magma Docks on Cindara. The crew has to fill the cargo bays of a whole fleet of lava freighters, and every crate count has to be right.

**Meters on Cindara:** Cargo loaded (starts at 10%) · Freighters launched · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. We're at the Magma Docks on Cindara, the lava world. A fleet of lava freighters is waiting to be loaded. Each one has cargo bays full of crates, and the numbers are big. Help Kai and Nova multiply fast and check every count. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Cinder cones | A cinder cone is a small, steep volcano. It is built from loose chunks of lava rock that pile up around the opening. |
| 2 | Slow lava | Many lava flows creep along slower than a person can walk. That usually gives people time to move out of the way. |
| 3 | Scoria | Scoria is a dark volcanic rock full of holes, a lot like pumice. But scoria is heavier, so it sinks in water. |
| 5 | The times sign | The × sign for multiplying was first printed in a math book by William Oughtred in 1631. |
| 6 | Pele's hair | Wind can stretch drops of lava into thin threads of volcanic glass. They are called Pele's hair, after a Hawaiian goddess of volcanoes. |
| 8 | Doubling to multiply | In ancient Egypt, people multiplied by doubling a number again and again, then adding some of the doubles together. |
| 10 | The volcano moon | Jupiter's moon Io has more active volcanoes than any other place in our solar system. |
| 11 | Giant cargo ships | The biggest cargo ships on Earth can carry more than 20,000 shipping containers at one time. |
| 13 | Long freight trains | Some freight trains on Earth are more than 3 kilometers (about 2 miles) long. |
