# The Tug-of-War — Content v1 (SCI.5.7A-XP)

**Grade 5 · Science · Frostveil.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/tug-of-war.js`.

**I can:** I can explain how equal and opposite forces act on objects, find the net force in newtons, and predict how unbalanced forces change motion. I can design a fair test of how force affects an object.

**Mission:** Measure forces, find the net force, run fair tests, and pick the tow plan that frees Rover Two.

**Story:** A snowdrift has trapped Rover Two on Frostveil, and a storm is on the way. The crew measures pulls in newtons, tests friction on ice and snow, and uses net force to plan the tow that sets the rover free.

**Meters on Frostveil:** Rover freed (starts at 10%) · Tow lines · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |

| 2 | One newton | Scientists measure force in newtons. A small apple resting in your hand pushes down with a force of about 1 newton. |
| 4 | Belly-sliding penguins | Penguins sometimes flop onto their bellies and slide across the snow and ice, pushing with their feet. This is called tobogganing, and it can be faster than waddling. |
| 5 | Spring scales | A spring scale measures force. When you pull on its hook, a spring inside stretches, and the harder you pull, the farther it stretches. |
| 6 | Coasting through space | The Voyager 1 space probe launched in 1977. Its main rocket stopped firing long ago, but with almost nothing to slow it down, it keeps coasting through space far beyond the planets. |
| 9 | Rocket launches | A rocket's engines blast hot gas down out of the bottom. The gas pushes back up on the rocket with an equal and opposite force, and that lifts it off the ground. |
| 10 | Bobsled starts | Bobsled teams push their sled as hard as they can for the first few seconds, then jump in. Gravity keeps pulling the sled down the icy track, so it speeds up as it goes. |
