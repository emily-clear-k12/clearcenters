# Exhibit Hall case list

**Updated Sept 24, 2026.** This wall gets **4 cases per subject per grade** (48). Check this file before writing one. If a code is already here, do not write it again.

Science codes have no prefix (`3.13A-EX`). ELAR uses `ELA.` and Social Studies uses `SS.`, so `ELA.3.8A-EX` is not science `3.8A-EX`.

This wall is four spots. Each spot is one job. A card is right when its job matches that spot. One myth. One late note. Photos, short text, or a bar graph.

Side-by-side, timelines, and drawn math stay off this wall. They are the next walls.

## What each open case is proving

**Science 3.** Meet water: four ways people use water. A plant in the rock: four clues that something once lived in a rock.

**Science 4.** When Texas was a sea: four fossil clues. Who eats whom: sun and producer, plant-eater, meat-eater, decomposer.

**Science 5.** Wolves: one change ripples through a food web. Landforms: delta, canyon, dunes, valley. Pond: four parts that keep it alive. Same job, different body: four animals, each structure matched to its job. Not a second desert case.

**ELAR 3.** Meet Rosa: four lines that prove what she is like. The sister's part: four lines that show how two characters treat each other. New story, not the kite again.

**ELAR 4.** Cracked pot: theme, not plot. Words that paint pictures: simile, metaphor, personification, and one tricky line. Leo: how he changes. How the article is built: sequence, cause and effect, description, question and answer.

**ELAR 5.** Monarchs: the author wants the reader to help. Who is speaking: the speaker is not the poet. Which sources belong: four sources, only the ones that answer the question. Tricks writers use: hyperbole and anecdote, handled carefully. Do not repeat a stereotype as the "right" card.

**Social Studies 3.** Good citizens: four acts. People changed this place: four human changes, not a flood. Franklin: four inventions, and the myth that he discovered electricity. Who does this job: four local government jobs.

**Social Studies 4.** Austin: four true things he did. Four ways of living in Texas: four American Indian nations and how each used the land. Not a repeat of Changing Texas.

**Social Studies 5.** Road to revolution: four causes, not the Declaration itself. Rights: four real protections. Lady Liberty: four true things she stands for. Four problems to solve: four compromises at the Constitutional Convention.
