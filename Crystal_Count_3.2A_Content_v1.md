# The Crystal Count — Content v1 (MA.3.2A-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Frostveil.** Written Sept 27, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/crystal-count.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.2A · **Also shows up under:** 3.2B, 3.2C

**I can:** I can build and break apart numbers with blocks, pictures, and expanded form. I can tell what a digit is worth and round numbers on a number line.

**Mission:** Bundle the ice crystals into rods, flats, and crates, and close the supply log.

**Story:** The ice caves of Frostveil sparkle with crystals, and the supply ship is coming. The crew has to count, bundle, and log every crystal before it leaves.

**Meters on Frostveil:** Log complete (starts at 25%) · Crates · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. We've reached the Crystal Caves on Frostveil. The caves are full of ice crystals, and the supply ship needs an exact count. Loose crystals get bundled into rods of 10, rods into flats of 100, and flats into crates of 1,000. Help Kai and Nova bundle every crystal and fill in the supply log. Vega out.
