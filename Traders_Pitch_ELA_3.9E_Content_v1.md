# The Trader's Pitch — Content v1 (ELA.3.9E-XP)

**Grade 3 · ELAR · Mechara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/traders-pitch.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.9E · **Also shows up under:** 3.10A, 3.6F, 3.7C, 3.3B, 3.11D, 3.12C

**I can:** I can find the claim in a pitch, tell facts from opinions, and name who the writer is talking to. I can write my own opinion with a reason.

**Mission:** Read each trader's pitch, find the claims, sort facts from opinions, and help Commander Vega make a smart deal.

**Story:** In the robot city of Mechara, traders line up to pitch gadgets to the crew. Ads, letters, and posters all promise something, but which parts are facts?

**Meters on Mechara:** Deal meter (starts at 10%) · Fact chips · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. We've landed at the Gear Street Market on Mechara. Word got out that we have fresh water and space crackers. Now every trader in the robot city wants to make a deal with us. Their pitches sound great. Help us find out which parts are facts and which are just opinions. Vega out.

### Passage E · Nova's Robot Review (Review)

Kai wanted a Super Scrub, so I tested one next to another cleaning bot, the Dust Buddy. `E1.1` I used a timer and a measuring cup for every test. `E1.2`

The Super Scrub cleaned our supply room in 20 minutes. `E2.1` The Dust Buddy took 35 minutes to clean the same room. `E2.2` Both robots picked up about two cups of dust. `E2.3`

The ad said the Super Scrub's battery lasts four hours. `E3.1` In my test, it lasted only two hours. `E3.2` The Dust Buddy's battery lasted five hours. `E3.3`

In my opinion, the Dust Buddy is the better robot for our ship. `E4.1` It is slower, but it can clean every room on one charge. `E4.2` My advice is simple: test what an ad says before you believe it. `E4.3`
