# The Fossil Dig — Content v1 (SCI.3.12D-XP)

**Grade 3 · Science · Cindara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/fossil-dig.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.12D · **Also shows up under:** 3.1E, 3.2B, 3.2C, 3.3A, 3.3C, 3.5A, 3.5B, 3.5G

**I can:** I can explain how fossils show what plants and animals lived long ago and what the land was like, such as sea fossils found on dry land.

**Mission:** Study Earth fossils and rock layers, and build a Fossil Hall that tells the story of long ago.

**Story:** Cindara's lava rock has no fossils, so Earth sends rock samples to the crew. They study real Earth fossils to find out what lived long ago and how places changed.

**Meters on Cindara:** Fossil hall built (starts at 20%) · Fossils cataloged · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. The rock on Cindara is cooled lava, and it holds no fossils. So Earth is sending us crates of rock with real fossils inside. We're going to build a Fossil Hall at the lab. Every fossil is a clue about life long ago. Look closely and follow the evidence. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Fossil poop | Fossil poop is called a coprolite. It is a trace fossil, and scientists can study it to find out what an animal ate. |
| 3 | Wood made of stone | Texas's state stone is petrified palmwood. It formed when minerals in water slowly filled and replaced the wood of ancient palm trees. |
| 5 | Dinosaur Valley | At Dinosaur Valley State Park near Glen Rose, Texas, you can see real dinosaur footprints in the rock of the Paluxy River. They were made about 113 million years ago in mud near an ancient sea. |
| 6 | Trapped in amber | Some insects are kept as fossils in amber. Amber is tree sap that trapped them millions of years ago and slowly hardened. |
| 7 | Sea fossils on Mount Everest | Rocks near the top of Mount Everest hold fossils of tiny sea animals. That rock formed on the floor of an ocean long ago and was slowly pushed up. |
| 8 | Trilobites | Trilobites were sea animals with hard shells and many legs. They lived for more than 250 million years and died out before the first dinosaurs. |
| 9 | A sea over Texas | Long ago, a shallow sea covered much of Texas. That's why fossils of sea animals, like coiled ammonite shells, are found across the state today. |
| 10 | A mammoth herd in Texas | At Waco Mammoth National Monument in Texas, scientists dug up fossils of a whole herd of Columbian mammoths from the Ice Age. |
| 11 | Forests in Antarctica | Scientists have found fossil leaves and tree trunks in Antarctica. Millions of years ago, Antarctica was much warmer and had forests. |
| 13 | Dinosaurs and people | The last big dinosaurs died out about 66 million years ago. The first people like us appeared only about 300,000 years ago. |
