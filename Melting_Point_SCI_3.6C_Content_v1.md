# The Melting Point — Content v1 (SCI.3.6C-XP)

**Grade 3 · Science · Cindara.** Written Sept 27, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/melting-point.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.6C · **Also shows up under:** 3.6D, 3.1B, 3.1D, 3.1E, 3.2B, 3.2C, 3.2D, 3.3A, 3.3C, 3.5B, 3.5G

**I can:** I can predict, observe, and record how heating and cooling change matter, like ice melting, water boiling into vapor, and drops forming on a cold glass. I can choose materials for a shelter based on how they act in heat.

**Mission:** Heat and cool materials, record what changes, and build a shelter that stays solid near the lava.

**Story:** The crew needs a shelter near Cindara's lava fields. Before they build, they have to find out what heat and cold do to ice, water, wax, chocolate, and stone.

**Meters on Cindara:** Shelter built (starts at 20%) · Test logs · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Melting and freezing point | Ice melts at 0°C (32°F). Liquid water freezes into ice at that same temperature. |
| 3 | Boiling point | At sea level, water boils at 100°C (212°F). Boiling water turns into water vapor, a gas you can't see. |
| 5 | Melting chocolate | Chocolate melts at about the same temperature as your body. That's why it can melt in your hand. |
| 6 | Back and forth | Water can change from ice to liquid and back again many times. Heating melts it, and cooling freezes it. It is still water. |
| 7 | Condensation | When water vapor in the air touches something cold, it cools and turns back into liquid drops. This is called condensation. |
| 9 | Lava becomes rock | Lava is melted rock. When it cools, it hardens into solid rock, such as basalt. |
| 10 | Ice on volcanoes | Some volcanoes on Earth, like ones in Iceland, are covered in ice. When they erupt, the heat melts the ice very fast and can cause floods. |
| 13 | Melted rock | Rock only melts when it gets extremely hot, about 700°C to 1,200°C. Melted rock underground is called magma. When it comes out of a volcano, it is called lava. |
| 14 | Morning dew | Dew on grass in the morning forms the same way. Water vapor in the air condenses on cool surfaces during the night. |
| 15 | Geysers | A geyser is a hot spring where hot rock underground heats water until it shoots up as hot water and steam. Old Faithful in Yellowstone is a famous one. |
