# The Web of Life — Content v1 (SCI.4.12B-XP)

**Grade 4 · Science · Solara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/web-of-life.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.12B · **Also shows up under:** 4.1G, 4.2B, 4.2C, 4.3A, 4.3B, 4.3C, 4.5B, 4.5D, 4.5E, 4.5G

**I can:** I can describe how energy flows from the Sun through a food web and how matter cycles back to the soil. I can explain the roles of producers, consumers, and decomposers.

**Mission:** Trace the paths in real Earth food webs, follow the Sun's energy one way, and show how decomposers send matter back around.

**Story:** From the treetop observatory on Solara, the crew points the telescope at real Earth food webs: a Texas salt marsh, a desert, a forest, and the Gulf of Mexico. They trace the paths, count the chains, and predict what happens when one living thing changes.

**Meters on Solara:** Web strength (starts at 30%) · Paths traced · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).
