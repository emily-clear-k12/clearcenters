# The Glow Garden Harvest — Outline v1 (for Emily's approval)

**Grade 3 · Math · Lumara · Quest code: MA.3.3AB-XP** (proposed)
**Focus:** 3.3A represent fractions with pictures, strip diagrams, and number lines · 3.3B name the fraction at a point on a number line
**Supporting:** 3.3C unit fractions (one equal part) · 3.3D a fraction is a sum of unit fractions · 3.7A fractions as distances on a number line
**Grade 3 limits kept:** denominators 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 only; every fraction is greater than 0 and no more than 1. No adding fractions with symbols; students count equal parts.

**I can:** I can show a fraction with pictures, strip diagrams, and number lines. I can find the fraction at a point on a number line.

**Mission:** Lumara's Glow Garden is getting ready for its harvest festival, but the garden's lights are going dim. Help Kai and Nova split the harvest into equal parts and light the path to the festival stage.

**Crew:** Commander Vega, Kai (engineer), Nova (scientist), S.A.M. (helper)
**Meters:** Glow (starts at 30%, like Heat on Frostveil) · Harvest baskets · Journal (10 discoveries)

## Act 1 · Split the harvest

**Opening (Vega):** "Specialist {name}, the Glow Garden on Lumara is dimming right before its harvest festival. The crew needs to split the harvest into equal parts and power the garden lights. Every basket, lantern, and path is measured in fractions."

| # | Level | Machine | Task | Answer | Wrong answers it catches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warm-up | Fill | **Kai:** "The glowberry juice tank has 4 equal parts. I filled 1 part. Set the gauge." | 1/4 | **1/3** (counted the lines inside the tank, not the parts) · **4/1** (flipped the numbers) |
| 2 | Warm-up | Crew Debate | **Kai:** "I cut this moon-leaf into 4 pieces, so each piece is 1/4!" (The pieces are different sizes.) **Nova:** "They aren't equal, so they aren't fourths." Who's right? | Nova. Fourths must be 4 **equal** parts. | **Kai** (any 4 pieces are fourths) |
| 3 | Warm-up | Tune | **Kai:** "The light beacon's dial goes from 0 to 1 in thirds. The signal is 2 jumps from 0. Set the dial." | 2/3 | **2/4** (counted the tick marks, including 0 and 1) · **1/3** (counted one jump) |
| 4 | Warm-up | Scoops | **Nova:** "Fill the nectar jar to 5/6. Our scoops hold 1/6 or 2/6. Fill it two different ways." | Any two different scoop sets that make 5/6 (e.g., 2/6 + 2/6 + 1/6, and five 1/6 scoops) | A set that makes 4/6 or 6/6 |
| 5 | **Challenge** | Fill (2 steps) | **Surprise, the lantern strip flickers!** The strip has 8 equal sections. Nova lit 3 sections, then Kai lit 2 more. Step 1: What fraction is lit? Step 2: What fraction is still dark? | 1. 5/8 · 2. 3/8 | **5/16** (added the bottom numbers, too) · **Step 2: 5/8** (answered the lit part again) |

**Act 1 complete:** "The harvest is split and the lanterns are glowing, {firstName}!"
