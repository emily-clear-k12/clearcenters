Mockup of the student My Progress page, using the demo class. Switch students to see how it changes. Kid colors stay the same as today: green Nailed It, teal Getting There, gold Keep Practicing (no red on the student side).
View as:
Starts with "what do I do now?"Try-agains, past-due and not-started missions sit in one row at the top, each with one big button. No hunting through three lists.
Skills, not just missions"My skills" turns the standards into "I can…" sentences with stars, so kids see what they're getting good at, and a Practice button when a skill needs work.
Always encouragingGrowth is told as "you're climbing" or "let's get back on track", never as slipping. "How sure were you?" teaches kids to trust (or double-check) themselves.
