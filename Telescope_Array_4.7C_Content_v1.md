# The Telescope Array — Content v1 (MA.4.7C-XP)

**Grade 4 · Math · Cloudreach.** Written Sept 27, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/telescope-array.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.7C · **Also shows up under:** 4.7A, 4.7B, 4.7D, 4.7E

**I can:** I can measure angles in degrees with a protractor, reading the right scale. I can draw an angle, sort angles by size, and find a missing part of two angles that sit side by side.

**Mission:** Measure and aim the sky reef's telescopes in degrees and lock onto the comet.

**Story:** A new comet is streaking past Cloudreach! The crew has to aim every telescope on the sky reef at just the right angle before it's gone.

**Meters on Cloudreach:** Comet lock (starts at 20%) · Telescopes aimed · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. We're on the Sky Reef, high above the clouds of Cloudreach. A brand-new comet is passing tonight! The reef has a whole array of telescopes, and each one has to turn to exactly the right angle. Grab a protractor and help Kai and Nova measure every angle in degrees. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Dirty snowballs | A comet is a big chunk of ice, dust, and rock. Scientists sometimes call comets "dirty snowballs." |
| 2 | Comet tails | When a comet gets close to the Sun, its ice starts to melt into gas. That makes a glowing tail, and the tail always points away from the Sun. |
| 3 | Galileo's telescope | In 1609, Galileo Galilei pointed a telescope at the night sky. He was one of the first people to see the Moon's craters and four of Jupiter's moons. |
| 5 | Halley's Comet | Halley's Comet comes back near Earth about every 76 years. It was last seen in 1986, and it will be back in 2061. |
| 6 | Our star | The Sun is a star. It looks bigger and brighter than other stars because it is so much closer to Earth. |
| 9 | Saturn's rings | Saturn's rings are made of billions of pieces of ice and rock. Some are as small as dust, and some are as big as a house. |
| 10 | Hubble Space Telescope | The Hubble Space Telescope was launched in 1990. It flies above Earth's air, so its pictures of space are very sharp. |
| 11 | Maria Mitchell | In 1847, Maria Mitchell found a new comet with her telescope. She became America's first woman to work as an astronomer. |
| 13 | The North Star | Polaris, the North Star, sits almost right above Earth's North Pole. It hardly seems to move, so travelers used it to find north. |
| 15 | Comet crash | In 1994, pieces of Comet Shoemaker-Levy 9 crashed into Jupiter. Telescopes all over Earth watched the dark spots they left behind. |
