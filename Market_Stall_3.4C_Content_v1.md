# The Market Stall — Content v1 (MA.3.4C-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Solara.** Written Sept 27, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/market-stall.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.4C · **Also shows up under:** 3.9C, 3.4A

**I can:** I can find the value of a group of coins and bills, make change, and think about the costs and benefits of spending choices.

**Mission:** Run the jungle market stall, count every coin and bill, and make the right change for every camper.

**Story:** The canopy camps on Solara's jungle moon trade supplies at a market stall. The crew needs help counting coins and bills, making change, and planning how to spend.

**Meters on Solara:** Market buzz (starts at 20%) · Crates traded · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. It's market day at the canopy camps on Solara. The camps share supplies at a market stall, and they practice trading with Earth dollars and cents. Help Kai and Nova count the money and make the right change for every camper. Vega out.
