# The Moon Watch — Content v1 (SCI.4.9B-XP)

**Grade 4 · Science · Lumara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/moon-watch.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.9B · **Also shows up under:** 4.1E, 4.1F, 4.2B, 4.2D, 4.3A, 4.3C, 4.5A, 4.5D

**I can:** I can collect and study Moon data to put the Moon's phases in order. I can use the pattern to predict how the Moon will look from Earth.

**Mission:** Log Earth's Moon for a month, put its phases in order, and use the pattern to predict the next full moon.

**Story:** From the observatory on Lumara, the crew watches Earth's Moon through a giant telescope for a whole month. They log its shapes, crack its pattern, and predict the date for the Moon Glow festival.

**Meters on Lumara:** Scope focus (starts at 25%) · Moon logs · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. The Glow Garden observatory on Lumara has a telescope strong enough to see Earth's Moon. The garden council wants to hold a Moon Glow festival on the next full moon. To pick the night, we need to know the Moon's pattern. For one month, we'll log what Earth's Moon looks like from Earth. Then we'll use the pattern to predict. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |

| 2 | Moon + month | The word "month" comes from an old word for "Moon." Long ago, people kept track of time by counting how many times the Moon went through its phases. |
| 5 | Earthshine | When the Moon is a thin crescent, you can sometimes see its dark part glowing faintly. That glow is sunlight that bounced off Earth, then off the Moon, and back to your eyes. It is called earthshine. |
| 6 | Lunar eclipses | Earth's shadow does fall on the Moon sometimes, but only during a full moon. That's a lunar eclipse. The Moon can turn coppery red, because Earth's air bends a little reddish sunlight onto it. |
| 7 | Upside-down Moon | People in Earth's Southern Hemisphere, like Australia, see the Moon flipped. For them, a waxing Moon is lit on the left side. |
| 8 | Full moon rising | A full moon rises in the east at about the same time the Sun sets in the west. It stays up all night and sets around sunrise. |
| 10 | Blue moon | A Moon cycle is a little shorter than most calendar months. So once every two or three years, a month has two full moons. The second one is called a blue moon, but it doesn't look blue! |
| 13 | The daytime Moon | The Moon isn't only a night sight. On many days of the month you can see it in the daytime sky, especially near the first quarter in the afternoon. |
| 14 | The late Moon | The Moon rises about 50 minutes later each day, on average. That's because it moves a bit farther along its orbit around Earth every day. |
| 15 | Harvest moon | The full moon closest to the first day of fall is called the harvest moon. Long ago, its bright light after sunset helped farmers keep bringing in their crops. |
