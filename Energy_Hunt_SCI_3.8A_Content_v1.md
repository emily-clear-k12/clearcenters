# The Energy Hunt — Content v1 (SCI.3.8A-XP)

**Grade 3 · Science · Lumara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/energy-hunt.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.8A · **Also shows up under:** 3.1B, 3.1D, 3.1E, 3.2B, 3.2C, 3.3A, 3.3C, 3.5B

**I can:** I can find everyday examples of light, sound, and thermal energy (heat) around me. I can tell that sound comes from vibrations, light comes from a source and lets us see, and heat moves from warmer things to cooler things.

**Mission:** Find and sort light, sound, and thermal energy, test how sound and heat work, and fill in the outpost's energy hunt map.

**Story:** The crew is on an energy hunt around Lumara's garden outpost. They track down light, sound, and heat in lamps, drums, cocoa, sunny spots, and a crackling campfire.

**Meters on Lumara:** Hunt map (starts at 20%) · Energy tags · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. Welcome to the garden outpost on Lumara. Energy is hiding all around us: in lamps, bells, warm cocoa, and sunny spots. Our job is to find it. Light, sound, and heat work here just like on Earth. Look, listen, and feel. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | Your voice | When you talk or sing, two small flaps in your throat called vocal cords vibrate. Put your fingers on your throat and hum to feel them buzz. |
| 5 | Body heat | Your body gives off thermal energy all the time. That's why a room full of people warms up, even with the heater off. |
| 6 | Heat on the move | Heat always moves from a warmer thing to a cooler thing. There is no such thing as 'cold' moving. Cold just means less heat. |
| 7 | Silent space | Sound needs something like air or water to travel through. Space has almost no air, so astronauts use radios to talk outside their spaceship. |
| 8 | Blowing on soup | Blowing on hot soup helps it cool faster. Moving air carries heat away from the soup more quickly. |
| 9 | Keep-warm bottles | A keep-warm bottle has a double wall with almost no air in between. That slows heat from moving out, so hot drinks stay hot for hours. |
| 10 | Cool tree shade | Trees shade the ground from sunlight. On a hot day, a shady park can feel much cooler than a sunny parking lot nearby. |
| 11 | Anglerfish | Deep in the ocean, where sunlight never reaches, anglerfish carry their own light. A glowing lure on the head attracts small fish to eat. |
| 13 | Chilly metal | A metal doorknob feels colder than a wooden door, even in the same room. Metal pulls heat out of your hand faster than wood does. |
| 15 | Sound in water | Sound travels about four times faster through water than through air. That's one reason whales can hear each other's songs from very far away. |
