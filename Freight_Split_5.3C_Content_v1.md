# The Freight Split — Content v1 (MA.5.3C-XP)

**Grade 5 · Math · Cindara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/freight-split.js`.

**Main standard:** 5.3C · **Also shows up under:** 5.3A, 5.3B

**I can:** I can divide a number up to four digits by a two-digit number, estimate with compatible numbers, check my answer with multiplication, and decide what to do with a remainder.

**Mission:** Split the lava-rock shipment into freighters, deal with every remainder, and clear the freight yard before the volcano rumbles.

**Story:** A giant shipment of lava rock is piling up at Cindara's freight yard. The crew has to split it into freighters, and every rock has to be counted exactly.

**Meters on Cindara:** Freight moved (starts at 15%) · Freighters loaded · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. We're at the Ember Freight Yard on Cindara, the lava world. The mines dug up a huge shipment of lava rock. It has to be split evenly into freighters and sent across the planet. Kai and Nova need a sharp divider. Estimate, divide, and check every load. Vega out.
