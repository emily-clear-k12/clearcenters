# The Lost Rover — Content v1 (ELA.3.8C-XP)

**Grade 3 · ELAR · Frostveil.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/lost-rover.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.8C · **Also shows up under:** 3.10B, 3.6F, 3.7C, 3.7D, 3.3B, 3.8B

**I can:** I can put story events in order. I can tell a story's conflict, turning point, and resolution.

**Mission:** Read Liko's story in four parts. Map its plot on the crew's plot board, compare it with a second story, and send the plots to Mission Control.

**Story:** A snowstorm hits icy Frostveil, and little rover Button doesn't come home. Follow young helper Liko's story from the first warning to the rescue.

**Meters on Frostveil:** Rover beacon (starts at 10%) · Trail flags · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega at Snowcap Station on Frostveil. Our youngest helper, Liko, wrote a story about the night our little rover got lost in a storm. Help us map its plot: what happens first, what goes wrong, and how it all works out. Vega out.

## Passages

### Passage A · The Lost Rover, Part 1: The Storm Warning (Story (beginning))

Liko was the youngest helper at Snowcap Station. `A1.1` Her favorite job was caring for Button, a little rover about the size of a sled. `A1.2` Every afternoon, Button rolled out to the ice fields to scoop up snow samples. `A1.3` It always came home before dark. `A1.4`

Kai had built Button with a green lamp on top. `A2.1` Whenever someone flashed a light at Button, its lamp blinked back the same number of times. `A2.2` "That way, we can always find it," Kai liked to say. `A2.3`

One afternoon, the sky turned the color of an old gray spoon. `A3.1` Commander Vega looked at the weather screen and frowned. `A3.2` "A big storm is coming," she said. "Everyone stays inside tonight." `A3.3`

Liko waited by the door. `A4.1` The sun sank low, and snow began to swirl. `A4.2` Button did not come home. `A4.3`

### Passage B · The Lost Rover, Part 2: Into the Wind (Story (the problem grows))

Liko checked the tracker screen. Button's dot was near Needle Ridge, and it was not moving. `B1.1` A red warning flashed: Button's battery was running low. `B1.2` If the battery ran out in the cold, Button might never wake up again. `B1.3`

Liko wanted to run outside and find Button right away. `B2.1` But the wind howled, and the snow was so thick that she could not see the flagpole by the door. `B2.2` Rushing into a storm alone would be foolish, a silly choice that could get her hurt. `B2.3`

So Liko asked Kai for help. `B3.1` Kai clipped one end of a long rope to his belt and the other end to hers. `B3.2` "We stay together, and we follow the rope line," he said. `B3.3` Step by step, they pushed into the wind. `B3.4`

### Passage C · The Lost Rover, Part 3: The Blinking Light (Story (turning point))

At Needle Ridge, the snow came up to Liko's knees. `C1.1` She called Button's name again and again, but the wind swallowed her voice. `C1.2`

Then Liko had an idea. `C2.1` She switched her headlamp on and off, one, two, three times. `C2.2` For a long moment, nothing happened. `C2.3`

Then, far to the left, a tiny green light blinked back three times. `C3.1` "There you are!" Liko shouted. `C3.2` Button was stuck in a deep snowdrift, with only its antenna poking out. `C3.3`

Liko and Kai dug with their gloves until Button's wheels were free. `C4.1`

### Passage D · The Lost Rover, Part 4: Home Again (Story (ending))

Kai carried Button, and Liko held the rope as they followed it back to the station. `D1.1` Inside, Nova plugged Button into the charger. `D1.2` Its screen flickered, and then it beeped a happy beep. `D1.3`

Commander Vega wrapped a warm blanket around Liko. `D2.1` "You were brave, and you were also smart," she said. "You asked for help." `D2.2`

The next day, Liko and Kai painted Button bright orange so it would be easy to spot in the snow. `D3.1` They made a new rule, too: Button must head home at the first sign of a storm. `D3.2` That night, Liko fell asleep with Button humming softly beside her bed. `D3.3`

### Passage E · One Block of Ice (Short story)

Oona and Bram were best friends at Snowcap Station. `E1.1` Every winter, the station held an ice sculpture contest. `E1.2` This year, only one big block of clear ice was left. `E1.3`

Oona wanted to carve a snow owl. `E2.1` Bram wanted to carve a sled dog. `E2.2` They both grabbed the block at the same time, and neither one would let go. `E2.3` "I saw it first!" they shouted together. `E2.4`

For two days, the friends did not speak to each other. `E3.1` Oona carved a tiny owl from a leftover chunk, but it looked lonely. `E3.2` Bram's little dog kept cracking. `E3.3`

On the morning of the contest, Bram knocked on Oona's door. `E4.1` "What if we carve a dog pulling a sled, with an owl riding on top?" he asked. `E4.2` Oona grinned. `E4.3` They carved all day, side by side. `E4.4`

Their sculpture did not win first prize. `E5.1` But it made everyone laugh, and Oona and Bram were friends again. `E5.2`

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Whiteouts | In a whiteout, blowing snow and clouds make everything look white. People can't see the ground or the sky, so it's easy to get lost even close to home. |
| 4 | Cold batteries | Batteries lose power faster when they get very cold. That's why a phone can shut off early on a freezing day. |
| 5 | Messages to Mars | Radio messages between Earth and Mars take from about 4 to 24 minutes to travel one way. So people can't steer a Mars rover like a toy car. They plan its drives ahead of time. |
| 6 | Snow search dogs | Some dogs are trained to find people buried in snow. They use their strong sense of smell and can search a snowy slope much faster than people can. |
| 7 | Quiet snow | Fresh, fluffy snow makes the world sound quieter. The tiny air spaces between snowflakes soak up sound. |
| 8 | A rover washed by wind | The Mars rover Opportunity ran on solar panels. Dust piled up on them, but gusts of wind sometimes blew the panels clean, so the rover kept exploring for about 14 years. |
| 10 | Penguin huddles | In freezing winter storms, emperor penguins huddle in a big crowd to share warmth. They slowly take turns moving from the chilly outside edge to the warm middle. |
| 11 | A city of ice | Every winter in Harbin, China, artists build a giant festival of buildings made from blocks of ice cut from a frozen river. At night, colored lights glow inside the ice. |
| 13 | Snow goggles | Long ago, Inuit people carved snow goggles from bone, antler, or wood. Thin slits let in just a little light, which protected their eyes from the bright glare off the snow. |
| 15 | Warm snow houses | An igloo is a dome built from blocks of packed snow. The snow traps air and holds in heat, so inside can be many degrees warmer than the windy cold outside. |
