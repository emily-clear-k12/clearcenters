# The Power Grid — Content v1 (SCI.4.8C-XP)

**Grade 4 · Science · Mechara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/power-grid.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.8C · **Also shows up under:** 4.8B, 4.1B, 4.1G, 4.2B, 4.3A, 4.3C, 4.5B, 4.5D

**I can:** I can show and explain that electrical energy moves in a closed path. I can explain how a circuit can make light and heat.

**Mission:** Wire the outpost's lights and heaters, find every gap in the loop, and get the outpost ready for Mechara's power grid.

**Story:** The crew's new outpost on Mechara is dark and cold. The crew has to wire the lights and heaters, sort conductors from insulators, and prove that electricity needs a closed loop.

**Meters on Mechara:** Grid charge (starts at 20%) · Circuit badges · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. We just landed at our new outpost on Mechara, the robot city. The lights are dark, and the heaters are cold. Nothing is wired yet. Electricity here works just like it does on Earth. Help Kai and Nova wire the outpost before night falls. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Light switches | A light switch opens or closes a gap in a circuit. Flip it on, and the loop closes so electrical energy can flow to the light. |
| 4 | Gold in electronics | Tiny bits of gold are used on the connectors inside computers and phones. Gold conducts electricity well and never rusts. |
| 5 | Glowing wire | Old-style light bulbs have a very thin wire inside called a filament. Electrical energy makes it so hot that it glows and gives off light. |
| 6 | Birds on power lines | A bird can sit on one power line safely because it isn't part of a complete loop. The electricity stays in the wire and doesn't flow through the bird. |
| 8 | Glass on power poles | Power poles use glass or ceramic pieces to hold up the wires. These insulators keep electricity from flowing into the pole. |
| 9 | Pot handles | Many metal pots have handles made of wood or plastic. The metal pot conducts heat to the food, and the handle insulates your hand. |
| 10 | Toaster wires | The wires inside a toaster glow orange when it's on. Electrical energy turns into so much thermal energy that the wires get hot enough to toast bread. |
| 11 | Water and electricity | Tap water has tiny bits of minerals in it that let electricity flow. That's why you should never touch plugs or switches with wet hands. |
| 13 | Holiday lights | In some old strings of holiday lights, all the bulbs were in one loop. If one bulb burned out, it made a gap and the whole string went dark. |
| 15 | LED lights | LED bulbs change more of their electrical energy into light and less into heat than old-style bulbs. That's why they stay cooler and use less energy. |
