# The Shape Workshop — Content v1 (MA.3.6A-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Mechara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/shape-workshop.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.6A · **Also shows up under:** 3.6B

**I can:** I can sort and name 2D shapes and 3D figures by their faces, edges, vertices, sides, and angles, and I can tell which groups a quadrilateral belongs to.

**Mission:** Name, count, and sort every shape in the Shape Workshop so the crew can build the Shape-Bot for the city parade.

**Story:** The robots of Mechara are built from shapes. The crew has to count faces, edges, and vertices, sort solids and panels, and name every part the right way.

**Meters on Mechara:** Robot built (starts at 5%) · Blueprints stamped · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

Every robot here is built from shapes: cubes, prisms, cylinders, cones, spheres, and flat panels. Help Kai and Nova count faces, edges, and vertices, and name every part the right way. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Where "geometry" comes from | The word geometry comes from old Greek words that mean earth measuring. Long ago, people used shapes to measure land. |
| 2 | Opposite faces of a die | On a standard die, the dots on opposite faces always add up to 7. The 1 is across from the 6. |
| 4 | Soccer ball shapes | A classic soccer ball is sewn from 32 flat pieces: 12 pentagons and 20 hexagons. When it is pumped up, it is almost a sphere. |
| 5 | Strong triangles | Many bridges and towers are built from triangles. A triangle frame keeps its shape when you push on it, but a square frame can be pushed into a slanted shape. |
| 6 | Stone columns | At the Giant's Causeway in Northern Ireland, about 40,000 stone columns stand side by side. Many have six sides. They formed long ago when lava cooled and cracked. |
| 7 | Round bubbles | A floating soap bubble pulls itself into a sphere. A sphere holds the most air inside the least amount of bubble skin. |
| 8 | Stop sign shape | Stop signs in the United States are octagons, with 8 sides and 8 vertices. No other road sign in the country uses that shape. |
| 10 | How shapes roll | Lay a cylinder on its side and push it, and it rolls in a straight line. A cone rolls around and around in a circle, turning on its point. |
| 11 | The Great Pyramid | The Great Pyramid in Egypt has a square base and 4 triangle faces. It was built about 4,500 years ago. |
| 15 | Earth's shape | Earth is not a perfect sphere. Because it spins, it bulges out a little around its middle, at the equator. |
