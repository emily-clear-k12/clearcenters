# The New Crew Member — Content v1 (ELA.3.8B-XP)

**Grade 3 · ELAR · Mechara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/new-crew-member.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.8B · **Also shows up under:** 3.8D, 3.8C, 3.6F, 3.7C, 3.7B, 3.3B, 3.11D

**I can:** I can explain how major and minor characters feel about each other and how their relationships change. I can tell how the setting changes what happens.

**Mission:** Read the story of Pip-9 in four parts, figure out how each character feels about Pip, and show how their relationships change.

**Story:** A small new robot named Pip-9 joins the crew on Sprocket Hill. Kai is thrilled, Nova is unsure, and grumpy old Crank wants him out of the way. Then the city's lights go out.

**Meters on Mechara:** Outpost lights (starts at 20%) · Friendship bolts · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega on Sprocket Hill, high above the robot city of Mechara. Our crew just got a new member. His name is Pip-9, and he is very small and very new. Kai loves him already. Nova isn't so sure. Help us understand our crew. Vega out.

## Passages

### Passage A · The New Crew Member, Part 1: The Crate (Story)

The Sprocket Hill Outpost sits high above the robot city of Mechara. `A1.1` One morning, a builder named Juno rolled a big crate through the door. `A1.2` "Your new crew member is here," she said. `A1.3`

The crate popped open with a hiss. `A2.1` Out rolled a small, round robot with a blue light in his chest. `A2.2` His left wheel squeaked with every turn. `A2.3` "Hello," he said in a tiny voice. "I am Pip-9." `A2.4`

Kai jumped up so fast that he knocked over a box of bolts. `A3.1` "A new crew member! This is the best day ever!" he shouted. `A3.2` He showed Pip every button, every screen, and every snack drawer in the outpost. `A3.3`

Nova stayed by her lab table with her arms crossed. `A4.1` She was hesitant, so she did not come any closer. `A4.2` "He is so small," she whispered to Vega. "How can he help us?" `A4.3`

Commander Vega knelt down so her eyes were level with Pip's light. `A5.1` "Everyone is new once," she said. "Take your time, Pip." `A5.2`

Before Juno left, she tapped Pip's chest. `A6.1` "Pip runs on his own battery, not on city power," she said. `A6.2` "And he can fit into places where bigger robots cannot go." `A6.3` Then Juno waved goodbye and rolled away. `A6.4`

### Passage B · The New Crew Member, Part 2: Too Many Mistakes (Story)

Pip wanted to be helpful, so he tried every job he could find. `B1.1` He tried to carry Nova's sample jars, but his squeaky wheel wobbled. `B1.2` Crash! Two jars broke on the floor. `B1.3`

Nova sighed and picked up the pieces. `B2.1` "Please just stand over there, Pip," she said. `B2.2` Pip's blue light turned dim, and he rolled slowly to the corner. `B2.3` He watched Nova work and wished he could do something right for her. `B2.4`

Just then, an old delivery bot named Crank clanked in with a cart of parts. `B3.1` Crank was the oldest robot on Sprocket Hill, and his paint was covered in rust spots. `B3.2` "Out of my way, little squeaker!" he grumbled when Pip rolled in front of his cart. `B3.3` "I have carried parts through the tunnels under this hill for eighty years. I don't have time for new robots." `B3.4`

Kai patted Pip on the head. `B4.1` "Don't worry about Crank. He's grumpy with everybody," Kai said. `B4.2` Then Commander Vega gave Pip a small job counting bolts. `B4.3` "Start small," she said kindly. "You'll get there." `B4.4`

### Passage C · The New Crew Member, Part 3: Lights Out (Story)

That evening, a huge dust storm swept over Mechara. `C1.1` Red dust swirled around the tall towers of the city. `C1.2` Then, with a loud pop, every light in the city went out. `C1.3`

The outpost was as dark as the inside of a closed box. `C2.1` The heavy doors ran on city power, so now they would not open. `C2.2` Crank had come back with a late delivery, and now he was stuck inside with the crew. `C2.3` The screens were black, the heaters were quiet, and the air was getting cold. `C2.4`

"The city's reset switch is in the power room under our hill," Vega said. `C3.1` "But the big door to the power room will not open without power." `C3.2`

Crank grumbled in the dark. `C4.1` "There is an old cable tunnel behind the supply shelf," he said. "It goes all the way to the power room." `C4.2` "But it is narrow. No big robot or person could ever fit." `C4.3`

Everyone turned to look at the only light in the room. `C5.1` It was Pip's small blue light, still glowing in his chest. `C5.2` "No," said Nova. "He is too new. What if he gets stuck?" `C5.3`

### Passage D · The New Crew Member, Part 4: Pip Saves the Day (Story)

Vega knelt beside Pip again. `D1.1` "You don't have to go," she said. "But we believe you can." `D1.2` Pip's light glowed a little brighter. "I will try," he said. `D1.3`

Pip squeezed into the cable tunnel. `D2.1` It was dark and dusty, and it split into many paths. `D2.2` "Go left at the third pipe!" Crank called through the opening. "Then roll down the ramp!" `D2.3` Pip followed Crank's directions, turn by turn. `D2.4`

At the end of the tunnel, Pip found a big red lever. `D3.1` He pushed with all his might, and the lever clicked down. `D3.2` All across Mechara, lights blinked back on, one tower at a time. `D3.3`

When Pip rolled out of the tunnel, the crew cheered. `D4.1` Nova knelt down and wiped the dust off his light. `D4.2` "I was wrong about you," she said. "Will you be my lab partner?" `D4.3`

Crank just huffed and rolled away with his cart. `D5.1` But the next morning, the crew found a small can of oil by the door. `D5.2` A note on it said, "For the squeaker. From C." `D5.3` Pip's wheel never squeaked again. `D5.4`

### Passage F · Nova's Log: My New Lab Partner (Log (personal narrative))

When Pip first rolled out of his crate, I did not think he could help us. `F1.1` He was small, and he broke two of my sample jars on his first day. `F1.2` I told him to go stand in the corner. `F1.3`

Then the lights went out, and Pip was the only one who could fit in the tunnel. `F2.1` I was scared for him, but he went anyway. `F2.2`

Now I know that small does not mean weak. `F3.1` Pip helps me in the lab every day, and I save him a spot at my table. `F3.2` I am glad he is on our crew. `F3.3`
