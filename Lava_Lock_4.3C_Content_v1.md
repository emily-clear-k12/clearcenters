# The Lava Lock — Content v1 (MA.4.3C-XP)

**Grade 4 · Math · Cindara.** Written Sept 26, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/`.

**Main standard:** 4.3C · **Also shows up under:** 4.3D, 4.3G

**I can:** I can tell if two fractions are equal by using a number line or by multiplying the top and bottom by the same number. I can compare fractions with different tops and bottoms.

**Mission:** Match the coolant levels and seal the lava lock.

**Story:** On Cindara, a giant stone gate called the lava lock holds back a river of lava. It's overheating, and every gauge at the station is marked a different way.

**Meters on Cindara:** Cooling (starts at 35%) · Crystals · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** Act 1 works with the task types already on the site. Acts 2–3 are written and graded but stay locked until the new task types are built.

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {name}, this is Commander Vega. The lava lock on Cindara is overheating. The coolant tanks and sensors there are all marked differently, so the readings don't agree. Help Kai and Nova match the fractions, and keep that lava where it belongs. Vega out.
