**Grade 5 · Science · Lumara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/instinct-files.js`.

**Main standard:** 5.13B · **Also shows up under:** 5.13A, 5.2B, 5.2C, 5.2D, 5.3A, 5.3B, 5.3C

**I can:** I can tell instincts from learned behaviors and explain how both help animals survive in their environment.

**Mission:** Sort the animal files, study real investigations, and explain how instinct and learning help animals survive.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A spider's first web | A spider can spin its very first web without ever seeing another spider do it. Some spider silk is stronger than a steel thread of the same thickness. |
| 2 | Playing dead | When an opossum is badly scared, it may fall over and play dead. This is an instinct it can't control, and it can last for minutes or even hours. |
| 3 | Coming home | Many female sea turtles swim back to the same beach where they hatched, or one very close to it, to lay their own eggs years later. |
| 4 | Chimp school | Young chimpanzees learn to fish for termites by watching their mothers. It can take them years of practice to get good at it. |
| 5 | Flying solo | Young common cuckoos fly thousands of miles from Europe to Africa on their first trip all alone. Their parents already left weeks earlier. |
| 6 | Born builders | Beavers are born with the urge to build dams. The sound of rushing water makes them pile up sticks and mud. Scientists saw beavers pile sticks on a speaker playing that sound! |
| 7 | Hook makers | New Caledonian crows bend twigs into hooks. They use the hooks to pull juicy grubs out of holes in trees. |
| 10 | Jar openers | Octopuses can learn to unscrew jar lids to reach food inside. Some have even learned to open a jar from the inside! |
| 11 | A surprise defense | The Texas horned lizard is the state reptile of Texas. When a coyote attacks, it can squirt a stream of bad-tasting blood from its eyes! |
| 13 | Smelling home | Salmon can use their sense of smell to find the very stream where they hatched, even after years in the ocean. |
