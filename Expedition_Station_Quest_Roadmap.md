# Expedition Station — Quest Roadmap

**Master list of every planned quest, Sept 26, 2026.** Check here before writing a new quest, and update the status when one is written.

## Rules that keep quests from repeating

1. **One main standard per quest.** No two quests share a main standard. Supporting standards can repeat across quests.
2. **Every title is unique.**
3. **One subject per quest.** Math, ELAR, and science never mix.
4. **Spread the planets.** Each planet fits certain topics:
   - **Frostveil** (ice world): cold, water, weather, measurement, winter animals
   - **Cindara** (lava world): heat, rocks, landforms, energy
   - **Lumara** (glowing garden): light, plants, insects, growing things
   - **Solara** (jungle moon): rainforest, animals, food webs, sharing supplies
   - **Cloudreach** (sky reef): clouds, rain, oceans, sky creatures
   - **Mechara** (robot city): robots, circuits, machines, money, data
5. **Science stays Earth-true.** Real materials, real animals, real Earth and space facts.
6. **ELAR passages are original** and fit the quest's world (logs, legends, letters, plays, poems, field guides, ads).

**Status key:** ✅ Live (Act 1 playable) · 🔒 Written, locked (waiting on task types) · ⬜ Planned

**Code format:** `MA.3.4K-XP` (math), `ELA.4.8A-XP` (ELAR), `SCI.5.9-XP` (science). The code's standard is the main standard.

## Notes

- **Not covered on purpose:** speaking and listening (ELAR 1), fluency (ELAR 4), independent reading (ELAR 5), and cursive. These belong in Broadcast Booth or live instruction.
- **Process standards** (math 3.1–5.1, science 1–5) run through every quest as supporting standards.
- **Adding a quest:** pick a ⬜ row, write it in `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/`, register it in `catalog.js`, run the answer-key check, and change its status here.
- **Changing a main standard:** make sure no other row uses it.
