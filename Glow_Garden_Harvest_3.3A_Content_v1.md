# The Glow Garden Harvest — Content v1 (MA.3.3A-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Lumara.** Written Sept 26, 2026 from the approved outline.

**Main standard:** 3.3A · **Also shows up under:** 3.3B, 3.3C, 3.3D, 3.7A

**Grade 3 limits:** Denominators 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 only. Every fraction is more than 0 and no more than 1. Students count equal parts; no adding fractions with symbols.

**I can:** I can show a fraction with pictures, strip diagrams, and number lines. I can find the fraction at a point on a number line.

**Mission:** Split the harvest into equal parts and light the path to the festival stage.

**Status:** Act 1 works with the task types already on the site. Acts 2–3 are written and graded but stay locked until the student screen supports Travel, Repair, Numberless, Sort Bay, point mode on Tune, and one-way scoops.

**Meters on Lumara:** Glow (starts at 30%) · Harvest baskets · Journal (10 discoveries)
