# The Cooling Core — Content v1 (SCI.5.6A-XP)

**Grade 5 · Science · Cindara.** Written Sept 26, 2026 (from the pilot in the Quest Blueprint doc). Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/`.

**Main standard:** 5.6A · **Also shows up under:** 5.6B, 5.6C, 5.6D, 5.1B, 5.1C, 5.2B, 5.3A

**I can:** I can compare materials by their properties, like magnetism, whether they float, their state, and whether they dissolve or conduct heat. I can separate mixtures and explain what happens in a solution.

**Mission:** Sort samples, recover spilled supplies, and choose the right material to rebuild the core's heat shield.

**Earth-true rule:** Every material in this quest is real and behaves as it does on Earth. Only the planet and crew are invented.

**Status:** Fully written and graded. Every act is locked and the quest is hidden from Assign until the student screen supports the ELAR/science task types (see `Expedition_Station_ELAR_Science_Task_Types.md`).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {name}, this is Commander Vega. A lava tremor cracked the cooling core on Cindara. Parts and samples are scattered everywhere, and the crew needs to rebuild the core's heat shield. Every material behaves just like it does on Earth. Use what you know. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |

| 2 | Pumice | Pumice is a volcanic rock so full of air bubbles that it can float on water. |
| 3 | Obsidian | Obsidian is volcanic glass. It forms when lava cools very quickly. |
| 4 | Basalt | Basalt is the most common volcanic rock on Earth. |
| 5 | Salt from seawater | People have made salt for thousands of years by letting seawater evaporate in shallow pools. |
| 6 | Dissolving | Salt dissolves faster in warm water than in cold water. |
| 8 | Hot air balloons | Hot air balloons rise because hot air is less dense than the cooler air around it. |
| 10 | Repeated trials | Scientists repeat tests several times so one odd result doesn't fool them. |
| 11 | Oven mitts | Oven mitts are made of materials that insulate, so heat can't easily reach your hand. |
| 13 | Icebergs | Icebergs float because ice is less dense than liquid water. Most of an iceberg is hidden under the water. |
