# The Signal Race — Content v1 (MA.3.2D-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Cloudreach.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/signal-race.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.2D · **Also shows up under:** 3.2C

**I can:** I can compare and order whole numbers up to 100,000 using >, <, and =. I can find a number on a number line and round it to the nearest ten, hundred, thousand, or ten thousand.

**Mission:** Compare, order, and round the sky-racers' signal scores to crown the Signal Race champion.

**Story:** It's race day on Cloudreach! Sky-racers zoom through the cloud gates and beam back signal scores. The crew has to compare, order, and round every score to crown a champion.

**Meters on Cloudreach:** Signal strength (starts at 15%) · Scores ranked · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).
