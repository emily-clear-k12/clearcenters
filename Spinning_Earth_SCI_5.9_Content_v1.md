# The Spinning Earth — Content v1 (SCI.5.9-XP)

**Grade 5 · Science · Cloudreach.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/spinning-earth.js`.

**Main standard:** 5.9 · **Also shows up under:** 5.1E, 5.1F, 5.1G, 5.2B, 5.2C, 5.3A, 5.3B, 5.3C, 5.5A, 5.5B

**I can:** I can show that Earth spins on its axis about once every 24 hours. I can explain how that spin makes day and night, makes the Sun seem to move across the sky, and changes where shadows point and how long they are.

**Mission:** Watch Earth spin, read the shadow-stick data, model day and night, and build a shadow clock you can explain.

**Story:** From the sky reef's big telescope, the crew watches Earth turn. A Texas partner station sends shadow-stick data, and the reef keepers want a shadow clock of their own.

**Meters on Cloudreach:** Sky clock (starts at 20%) · Shadow marks · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Earth's spin speed | At the equator, the ground spins with Earth at about 1,000 miles per hour. We don't feel it because we, the ground, and the air all move together. |
| 2 | A star day | Measured against faraway stars, Earth spins once in about 23 hours and 56 minutes. Our 24-hour day is a few minutes longer because Earth also moves along its path around the Sun. |
| 3 | Why things rise in the east | Earth spins toward the east. That's why the Sun, the Moon, and the stars all seem to rise in the east and set in the west. |
| 4 | Jupiter's short day | Jupiter spins faster than any other planet in our solar system. One day on Jupiter lasts only about 10 hours. |
| 5 | Texas noon shadows | Texas is too far north for the Sun to ever be straight overhead. So at midday, shadows in Texas point north all year long. |
| 6 | Foucault's pendulum | In 1851, Léon Foucault hung a heavy ball from a very long wire in Paris. Hour by hour, the direction of its swing slowly turned. That showed people Earth really spins. |
| 7 | The day-night line | The edge between the day side and the night side of Earth is called the terminator. From space, astronauts can watch it slide across Earth as Earth turns. |
| 10 | Lights on the night side | From space, the night side of Earth sparkles with city lights. Astronauts can trace coastlines and big cities just by their glow. |
| 13 | Almost no shadow | In Hawaii, the Sun is straight overhead at midday on a couple of days each year. People standing outside have almost no shadow. Hawaiians call it Lahaina Noon. |
| 15 | Shorter days long ago | Earth's spin is slowing down very, very slowly. Back when dinosaurs lived, one day on Earth was about half an hour shorter than it is today. |
