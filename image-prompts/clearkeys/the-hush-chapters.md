# ClearKeys story "The Hush" — chapter pictures

One picture per chapter. The student ClearKeys page shows the newest unlocked chapter as a big tile with its picture, and each chapter page uses it as its banner. Until a picture is made, the ClearKeys station picture shows instead.

These go in the Maker drop folder (`public/maker/the-hush/`), so students can also use them in Maker Studio.

## Keep the cast the same in every picture

- **Iris:** an 11-year-old girl, curly dark hair in two puffs, a teal jumpsuit; brave, funny and a little bossy.
- **Captain Ruth:** Iris's grandmother, the ship's captain; short silver hair, a lavender flight jacket, and a warm smile.
- **Gus:** a small round repair robot, white with teal trim, one antenna, big friendly eye-screen; dramatic and afraid of the dark.
- **S.A.M.:** the relay station's helper robot (use the existing S.A.M. look from ClearCenters).
- **The Kestrel:** a small rounded ship with large, shimmering starlight sails.
- **The Hush:** a huge soft dark cloud; in chapters 20–24 it is revealed as Echo, a baby starwhale made of starlight and dust.
- **The relay station:** a bright crystal space station with a glowing relay dish.

House style: soft crystal sci-fi, pastel lavender, white and teal with gentle glow; friendly and calm (never scary); kid-friendly for Grades 3–5; no text or letters in the image.
