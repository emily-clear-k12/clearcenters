# The Pollen Scoops — Content v1 (MA.4.3A-XP)

**Grade 4 · Math · Lumara.** Written Sept 26, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/pollen-scoops.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.3A · **Also shows up under:** 4.3B

**Grade 4 limits:** Denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 only. Most answers are no more than 1. The final challenge uses one small improper fraction (7/4 = 1 3/4).

**I can:** I can break a fraction into a sum of smaller fractions with the same bottom number, like 5/8 = 1/8 + 1/8 + 3/8. I can show the same fraction as a sum in more than one way.

**Mission:** Mix pollen for the glow bees, one scoop at a time, and get the hive ready for the queen's festival.

**Story:** On Lumara, the glow bees light up the garden at night, but only if they eat the right pollen mix. The crew has to mix it one scoop at a time.

**Meters on Lumara:** Hive glow (starts at 35%) · Honey jars · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task uses a task type already on the site and is graded.

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {name}, this is Commander Vega. The glow bees of Lumara are getting dim. They light up the garden at night, but only when they eat the right pollen mix. Every mix is made one scoop at a time. Help Kai and Nova add up the scoops so the hive glows again. Vega out.
