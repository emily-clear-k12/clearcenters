# The Creature Pens — Content v1 (MA.3.7B-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Solara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/creature-pens.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.7B · **Also shows up under:** 3.6C

**I can:** I can find the perimeter of a polygon by adding all its side lengths, find a missing side when I know the perimeter, and tell perimeter apart from area.

**Mission:** Measure and fence every rescue pen, find missing sides, and give the animals the most room for release night.

**Story:** The crew runs an animal rescue camp in the jungle on Solara. They have to fence pens for capybaras, tapirs, sloths, tortoises, and parrots before the animals move to their forest homes.

**Meters on Solara:** Pens ready (starts at 20%) · Pens built · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).
