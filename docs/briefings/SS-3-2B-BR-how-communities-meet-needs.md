## Phase A — Intel Drop (mystery open)

**Artifact:** Split collage — two unlabeled towns. Left: dirt road, one-room school, town-meeting porch, creek park, bulletin board. Right: ferry, school bus, mayor’s office, indoor rec, radio tower. Do **not** label the towns on the image.

**On-screen title only:** “What does this town take care of?”

**S.A.M. opener:**  
“Don’t name the towns yet. What do people here need taken care of?”

**Student moves:** chip-only claim.  
Frame: `I think this town takes care of __________.`

**Claim chips:** rules · school · messages · getting around · play · food

**Reveal:**  
“Today’s Intel Drop is really about **how communities meet needs**. Keep your claim — HQ wants to compare two towns, not pick a winner.”

**Learning target (pin after reveal):**  
Communities meet needs for **government**, **education**, **communication**, **transportation**, and **recreation**. Different towns can meet the **same need** in **different ways**.

---

## Phase B — Field Brief (direct teach)

### Vocab cards
| Term | Kid-friendly meaning |
|------|----------------------|
| government | people and rules that help a town run fairly |
| education | how kids learn (school, teachers, getting to class) |
| communication | how people send news and messages |
| transportation | how people and goods get from place to place |
| recreation | how people play and rest (parks, games, fun together) |

### Teach beats (3 cards — all five needs named)

**Beat 1 — Government and education**  
Every town needs a way to make fair rules, and a way for kids to learn. **Maple Crossing** holds a town meeting on the porch and kids **walk** to a small school. **Cloudreach** has a mayor’s office and kids ride a **school bus**. Same needs. Different ways.  
*Picture prompt:* porch meeting + walking kids | mayor’s office + yellow bus  
*QC:* Walking to school vs riding a bus is mostly which need? → Education

**Beat 2 — Communication and transportation**  
People need news, and they need to get around. Maple Crossing posts notes on a **bulletin board** and uses a **wagon road**. Cloudreach uses a **radio tower** and a **river ferry**.  
*Picture prompt:* bulletin board + wagon | radio tower + ferry  
*QC:* A ferry that carries people across the river is mostly which need? → Transportation

**Beat 3 — Recreation (and the compare move)**  
People also need a place to play and rest. Maple Crossing has a **creek park**. Cloudreach has an **indoor rec center**. That’s **recreation** — same need, different way. HQ’s job is not “which town is better.” It’s “how do they each take care of people?”  
*Picture prompt:* creek park vs indoor gym  
*QC:* True/False: Every town has to meet needs the exact same way. → False

**Teacher note:** Keep “other community” as Cloudreach (sister town), not a real named Texas town, so teachers can later swap in their own city.

## Phase C — Need Sort

Three bins (player is a 3-column sort): **Government** · **Education** · **Transportation**  
Communication and recreation stay in Field Brief + Clearance so the 30-minute spine doesn’t grow a 5-column sort.

**Items (2 per bin)**
- Town meeting on the porch to make fair rules → government
- A mayor’s office that helps the town run → government
- Kids walk down the lane to a small school → education
- Kids ride a yellow bus to a bigger school → education
- A wagon road to carry people and goods → transportation
- A river ferry instead of a long wagon trip → transportation

---

## Phase D — Ops Choice: Sister Town Signal

**Title:** Sister Town Signal — Maple Crossing & Cloudreach  
**Constraint:** HQ can compare **only two** needs this year. Three are real community needs. One is just for show.

**Projects (pick 2)**
1. Town meeting vs mayor’s office → government  
2. Walk to school vs school bus → education  
3. Wagon road vs river ferry → transportation  
4. World’s largest snack statue contest → distractor (not a need)

**Waiting:** the third real need waits until next year (same fund-meter as 3.2A).

**Debrief:** Both towns meet the need. They just do it differently. Fun-only projects are not a community need.

---

## Phase E — Evidence Drop

**Title:** Compare postcard to HQ  
Front: pick one scene (government / education / transportation).  
Back frame: `Both towns meet the need for __________. Maple Crossing does it by __________. Cloudreach does it by __________.`

Carry forward the two funded compares; kid picks one to show.

---

## Phase F — Clearance

1. What five needs do communities take care of? (government, education, communication, transportation, recreation)  
2. Walk vs bus — which need? Education  
3. T/F: Every town has to meet needs the exact same way. False  
4. Dynamic: which need is HQ still waiting to compare? / which need matches the postcard

**Cleared:** “Briefing cleared: How Communities Meet Needs. Ask your teacher when you’re ready for a Challenge.”
