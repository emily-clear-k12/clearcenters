## Phase A — Intel Drop (mystery open)

**Artifact (generate or stock):** Photo/collage of a locked community gate with a “Rules Posted” sign **or** unlabeled collage: school + market + house of worship.  
**On-screen title only:** “Why is this place here?”

**S.A.M. opener:**  
“Don’t tell me what this *is* yet. What do you **notice**? What do you **wonder**? Drop a claim: *I think this place exists because…*”

**Student moves**
1. Notice (optional chip or 1 short line)
2. Wonder (optional)
3. **Claim** (required): sentence or chip+finish  
   Frame: `I think this place exists because __________.`

**Claim chips (optional starters):** safety · rules · beliefs · food/jobs · school · friends · other

**Reveal (after submit):**  
“Today’s Intel Drop is really about **why people form communities**. Keep your claim — we’ll see if HQ’s three reasons match what you noticed.”

**Learning target (pin after reveal):**  
People form communities for **security and laws**, **religious freedom**, and **material well-being**.

### Server — Intel Drop scoring (not client-visible)
- Fail if blank / under 3 meaningful words

## Phase B — Field Brief (direct teach)

### Vocab cards
| Term | Kid-friendly meaning |
|------|----------------------|
| community | people living and working together in a place |
| security | staying safe and protected |
| laws | rules that help keep people safe and fair |
| religious freedom | being able to practice your beliefs |
| material well-being | having what you need to live well (food, homes, jobs, goods) |

### Teach beats (3 cards)

**Beat 1 — Security and laws**  
Living together helps people stay safe. Communities make **laws** (rules) so people know what’s fair and what keeps everyone protected.  
*Picture prompt:* stop sign / firefighter / posted rules  
*Example:* A town has firefighters and speed limits near schools.

**Beat 2 — Religious freedom**  
Some communities formed so people could practice their beliefs without being stopped.  
*Picture prompt:* simple diverse houses of worship / “freedom to believe”  
*Example:* Groups in U.S. history moved to new places for religious freedom.  
**Teacher note:** Keep civic and respectful — freedom to believe, not comparing religions.

**Beat 3 — Material well-being**  
People gather where they can get food, homes, jobs, and goods.  
*Picture prompt:* market / homes / river town  
*Example:* A town grows near a river for water, trade, and jobs.

### Quick checks (auto-graded, woven after beats)

**QC1** (after Beat 1)  
Speed limits near a school mostly match which reason?  
- A) Security and laws ✅  
- B) Religious freedom  
- C) Material well-being  

**QC2** (after Beat 2)  
A group moves so they can worship in their own way. Which reason?  
- A) Security and laws  
- B) Religious freedom ✅  
- C) Material well-being  

**QC3** (after Beat 3)  
Families settle near farms and markets for food and work. Which reason?  
- A) Security and laws  
- B) Religious freedom  
- C) Material well-being ✅  

**Trap line to surface (S.A.M. optional):**  
“Material well-being just means having toys.” → False; it’s needs like food, homes, jobs, goods.

## Phase C — Ops Choice (micro-simulation)

**Title:** Founders’ Council — Maple Crossing  
**Constraint:** The new town can fund **only two** of three big projects this year.

**Projects**
1. **Town hall + community rules** → Security and laws  
2. **Land set aside for a place of worship for anyone who wants it** → Religious freedom  
3. **Market + road so farmers can sell food and goods** → Material well-being  

**Student task**
1. Pick exactly **two** projects (multi-select; enforce count = 2)  
2. Justification (required): chips and/or short why  
   Chips: `We need to stay safe` · `People should be free to believe` · `People need food, homes, and jobs` · `We can add the third later`

**Debrief (always show all three TEKS reasons):**  
“Which reasons did Maple Crossing put first? Which waited? Communities form for **security and laws**, **religious freedom**, and **material well-being** — towns just can’t do everything on day one.”

**Design note:** No single “correct” pair. Success = valid two picks + justification that uses TEKS reason language.

### Server — Ops Choice scoring
- Auto-fail if not exactly 2 projects selected  
- Pass justification if it maps to at least one TEKS reason (keyword/chip)  
- Record which project was deferred for teacher insight (not shown as “wrong”)

## Phase D — Evidence Drop (teach-back)

**Product:** Community Postcard from Maple Crossing  

**Front:** Simple draw/upload or sticker scene that clearly shows **one** reason (rules sign, worship place, or market/homes).  
**Back (required frame):**  
`People formed our community for __________. You can see it because __________.`

**Dropdown or chips for blank 1:** security and laws · religious freedom · material well-being  
**Blank 2:** short evidence sentence or matching chip  
Evidence chips examples:  
- posted rules / firefighters / speed limits  
- place to worship freely  
- market, farms, jobs, homes  

**Publish:** optional 3-volunteer share in whole-group; in center mode, save to student notebook / class wall when that exists.

### Server — Evidence Drop rubric (`mustInclude`)
1. Names one of the three TEKS reasons accurately  
2. Evidence/example matches that reason (not mismatched)  
3. (Stretch) Uses the word *community* correctly  

**2-point teacher-facing score**  
- 2 = reason + matching evidence  
- 1 = reason without evidence, or evidence without clear reason  
- 0 = blank / off-topic  

AI grading prompt seed: score against mustInclude; never invent TEKS beyond 3.2A; keep feedback grade-3 kind and specific.

## Phase E — Clearance

**Objective items (auto)**

1. What three reasons does TEKS 3.2A name for forming communities?  
   - ✅ security and laws; religious freedom; material well-being  
   - (distractors mix “fun only,” “toys,” “being famous,” etc.)

2. A town hires firefighters and posts fire-safety rules. Best match?  
   - ✅ Security and laws  

3. True/False: Material well-being is only about having toys.  
   - ✅ False  

4. Settlers choose a place so they can practice their faith freely. Best match?  
   - ✅ Religious freedom  

5. In Founders’ Council, why might a town pick the market *and* the rules first?  
   - ✅ Sample key: people need to live well *and* stay safe; the third reason can wait  
   - Accept any answer that names two TEKS reasons without inventing a fourth required reason  

**Self-check (require 3 of 5)**  
1. Did I name security and laws?  
2. Did I name religious freedom?  
3. Did I name material well-being?  
4. Did my Ops Choice pick exactly two projects and say why?  
5. Did my postcard reason match my evidence?
