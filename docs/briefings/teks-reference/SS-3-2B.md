# SS-3-2B — How Communities Meet Needs

## Official standard text (current, 2022-adopted TEKS)

> Compare ways in which people in the local community and other
> communities meet their needs for government, education, communication,
> transportation, and recreation.

Verified against the current Texas Administrative Code text (19 TAC
§113.14, Social Studies Grade 3). **This one needed the cross-check**: the
first lead4ward mirror found (a district's re-hosted copy of the Grade 3
Social Studies TEKS Snapshot) still had the OLD "identify ways in which
people..." wording. Texas re-adopted the Social Studies TEKS in 2022 and
changed 3.2B's tested verb from "identify" to "compare" — that particular
mirror hadn't been updated to reflect it. Emily's existing
`SS-3-2B-BR.public.js` already uses "Compare," so it's correct as-is; this
is flagged here so a future automated research step doesn't get fooled by
the same stale mirror and regress a lesson's wording.

## Notes for an AI generation pass

- Same teksguide.org gap as SS-3-2A: no Social Studies coverage there.
- This standard's verb is "compare" — a generated lesson needs an actual
  point of comparison (this town vs. another community, or "your
  community" vs. "a community somewhere very different"), not just five
  facts about one place. That's a structural requirement the standard's
  own wording implies but a generator working only from a keyword list
  could easily miss.
- **General lesson for the pipeline, from finding the stale mirror**:
  treat lead4ward/teksguide snapshots as good for vocabulary, STAAR
  classification, and pedagogical framing — but always verify the exact
  standard wording itself against the official Texas Administrative Code
  text (regulations.justia.com or law.cornell.edu mirror it and aren't
  robots-blocked), especially for any standard in a subject that's been
  through a TEKS revision recently (Science 2024-2025; Social Studies
  2022).
