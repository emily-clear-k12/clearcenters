# The Trusted Source — Content v1 (ELA.5.13D-XP)

**Grade 5 · ELAR · Mechara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/trusted-source.js`.

**Main standard:** 5.13D · **Also shows up under:** 5.13C, 5.13E, 5.13F, 5.13G, 5.9E, 5.6F, 5.6H, 5.7C

**I can:** I can tell primary sources from secondary sources, decide which sources are credible, and use the best sources to make a recommendation.

**Mission:** Sort the sources, check each one for proof, bias, and dates, and help the city council choose a fix backed by the sources you trust most.

**Story:** In the robot city of Mechara, the lights keep flickering. A measurement log, an ad, a rumor, an encyclopedia, a news report, and an old guide all give different answers.

**Meters on Mechara:** City power (starts at 25%) · Source cards · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega in the robot city of Mechara. The lights in the Gearford District flicker every evening. The city council has to choose a fix, and six sources all say different things. Some are trustworthy. Some are not. Help us figure out which sources to trust. Vega out.

## Passages

### Passage A · Engineer Pell-7's Measurement Log (Measurement log (primary source))

Measurement Log, Substation 4, Gearford District, May 2031. `A1.1` Written by Engineer Pell-7, City Power Team. `A1.2` I am recording what I measure with my own meters, every day, at the same times. `A1.3`

May 3: At 5:00 p.m., the voltage on the main cable read 240 volts, which is normal. `A2.1` At 6:00 p.m., the street lamps switched on and about 4,000 robots plugged in to recharge. `A2.2` The voltage dropped to 205 volts, and lights across the district flickered for 14 minutes. `A2.3`

May 4: I measured again and saw the same pattern. `A3.1` The flickers began at 6:02 p.m. and stopped when most robots finished charging. `A3.2` At noon, when few robots were charging, the lights never flickered once. `A3.3`

May 5: I checked the cable itself. `A4.1` It is the original cable from when Substation 4 was built, and it was warm to the touch at 6:10 p.m. `A4.2` My data shows the flickers happen when too many robots charge at the same time. `A4.3`

### Passage D · Power Grids (Reference entry (secondary source))

A power grid is a network of wires that carries electricity from power stations to homes, shops, and charging stations. `D1.1` Along the way, electricity passes through substations, which control how the power flows to each neighborhood. `D1.2`

Every wire can safely carry only a certain amount of electric current. `D2.1` When too many devices use power from the same wire at once, the wire can become overloaded. `D2.2` An overloaded wire heats up, and the voltage drops, which can make lights dim or flicker. `D2.3`

The busiest time of day on a power grid is called peak demand. `D3.1` Engineers can reduce overloads by adding thicker cables or by spreading out the times when devices use power. `D3.2`

From the Mechara Library Encyclopedia. `D4.1` Entry reviewed by the Mechara Institute of Engineering. `D4.2` Last updated: January 2031. `D4.3`

### Passage E · Flickering Lights Puzzle Gearford (News report (secondary source))

Mechara Daily News, May 6, 2031. `E1.1` By Rina Bolt, science reporter. `E1.2`

For two weeks, lights in the Gearford District have flickered almost every evening. `E2.1` The trouble began soon after 30 new fast-charging stations opened in the district in April. `E2.2`

"Our meters show the flickers start when thousands of robots charge at once," said Engineer Pell-7 of the City Power Team. `E3.1` Dr. Ohma Watt, who studies power grids at the Mechara Institute of Engineering, agreed. `E3.2` "An old cable can only carry so much current," she explained. `E3.3`

Not everyone agrees. `E4.1` "I think it's that museum magnet," said one shopper, who did not give her name. `E4.2` The city council will vote next week on how to fix the problem. `E4.3`

### Passage F · Gearford District Power Guide (Guidebook (old edition))

Gearford District Power Guide, First Edition, 2004. `F1.1` Published by the City Power Team. `F1.2`

Substation 4 serves about 500 homes and shops. `F2.1` Its main cable can carry more than twice the power the district needs, even at the busiest hour. `F2.2`

Most robots in Gearford recharge once a week, usually on weekends. `F3.1` Because of this, flickering lights should never be a problem in this district. `F3.2` If your lights flicker, check the bulb first. `F3.3`

Lights may also flicker during big storms. `F4.1` Call the City Power Team if a problem lasts more than one day. `F4.2` Keep this guide in a safe place for future use. `F4.3`
