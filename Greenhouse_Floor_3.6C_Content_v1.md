# The Greenhouse Floor — Content v1 (MA.3.6C-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Lumara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/greenhouse-floor.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.6C · **Also shows up under:** 3.6D, 3.6E

**I can:** I can find the area of a rectangle by multiplying the number of rows by the number of unit squares in each row, split L-shapes into rectangles, and share a shape into equal-area parts.

**Mission:** Tile the greenhouse floor, plan the garden plots, and get the greenhouse glowing for glow night.

**Story:** The crew is building a new greenhouse in the glowing garden on Lumara. They have to tile the floor with square meters and plan fair garden plots before glow night.

**Meters on Lumara:** Floor tiled (starts at 20%) · Garden plots · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).
