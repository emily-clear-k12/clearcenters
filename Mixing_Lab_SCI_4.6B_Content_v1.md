# The Mixing Lab — Content v1 (SCI.4.6B-XP)

**Grade 4 · Science · Cloudreach.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/mixing-lab.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.6B · **Also shows up under:** 4.6C, 4.1B, 4.1D, 4.2B, 4.3A, 4.3C, 4.5B, 4.5E

**I can:** I can investigate and compare mixtures, including solutions of liquids in liquids and solids in liquids. I can separate mixtures and show that matter is conserved when mixtures form.

**Mission:** Sort mixtures and solutions, separate spilled materials, prove that dissolved matter still has mass, and mix a plant-food solution for the reef garden.

**Story:** A big gust scrambled the Mix Deck on Cloudreach's sky reef. The crew's lab uses real Earth materials, and the reef garden is waiting for supplies.

**Meters on Cloudreach:** Mix Deck (starts at 30%) · Sample jars · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. A big gust just rattled the Mix Deck on Cloudreach's sky reef. Labels blew away, jars tipped over, and the reef garden is waiting for supplies from our lab. Everything in the lab is a real Earth material, and it behaves just like it does on Earth. Use what you know. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Air is a mixture | Air is a mixture of gases. It is mostly nitrogen, and about one part in every five is oxygen. |
| 2 | The Dead Sea | The Dead Sea, between Israel and Jordan, is almost ten times saltier than the ocean. So much salt is dissolved in it that people float easily on top. |
| 5 | Panning for gold | Gold is very heavy for its size. Miners swirl sand and water in a pan, and the gold settles to the bottom while the lighter sand washes away. |
| 6 | The careful weigher | In the 1700s, a French scientist named Antoine Lavoisier weighed materials very carefully before and after they changed. He showed that matter is not lost. It only changes form. |
| 8 | Small pieces win | Crushed sugar dissolves faster than a sugar cube. Smaller pieces have more surface touching the water. |
| 9 | Oil spill booms | Oil is less dense than water, so it floats. After an oil spill, cleanup crews circle the oil with floating barriers called booms. |
| 10 | Rock candy | Hot water can dissolve much more sugar than cold water. Rock candy grows when a hot, super-sweet sugar solution cools and sugar crystals form on a string or stick. |
| 11 | Sand filters | Many water treatment plants clean drinking water by letting it trickle through thick layers of sand and gravel that trap tiny bits of dirt. |
| 13 | Soil is a mixture | Soil is a mixture. It has tiny bits of rock, bits of dead plants and animals, water, and air. |
| 15 | Epsom salt | Epsom salt is named after Epsom, a town in England. Long ago, people found it dissolved in the water of a spring there. |
