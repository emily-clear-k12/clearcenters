# The Light Lab — Content v1 (SCI.5.8C-XP)

**Grade 5 · Science · Lumara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/light-lab.js`.

**Main standard:** 5.8C · **Also shows up under:** 5.1A, 5.1B, 5.1G, 5.2B, 5.2D, 5.3A, 5.3C

**I can:** I can show that light travels in a straight line until it hits something, and then it is reflected, refracted, absorbed, or passes through.

**Mission:** Trace light beams, test materials, bend light with water and prisms, and use mirrors to send light around the garden wall.

**Story:** Lumara's glowing garden has a light lab full of mirrors, lenses, prisms, and water tanks. The crew needs to understand real light to keep the garden growing.

**Meters on Lumara:** Beam path (starts at 20%) · Test cards · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. Welcome to the light lab in Lumara's glowing garden. The garden's rare plants need light in just the right places, and the crew needs to learn how light moves. Every beam here behaves just like light on Earth. Use what you know. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Pinhole cameras | A pinhole camera is a dark box with a tiny hole. Light travels straight through the hole and makes an upside-down picture on the back wall. |
| 5 | Glowing cat eyes | Cats' eyes seem to glow at night because a shiny layer at the back of each eye reflects light. The eyes don't make any light of their own. |
| 6 | Ibn al-Haytham | About 1,000 years ago, a scientist named Ibn al-Haytham did experiments showing that we see when light travels from objects into our eyes, not out of our eyes. |
| 7 | Archerfish | Archerfish shoot jets of water to knock bugs off leaves above the water. They hit their target even though light bends where the water meets the air. |
| 8 | Diamond sparkle | Diamonds flash with tiny colors because they bend light even more strongly than glass or water does. |
| 9 | Glow-in-the-dark toys | Glow-in-the-dark toys soak up light energy. Then they give it off slowly, so they keep glowing for a while after the lights go out. |
| 10 | Cool roofs | Some buildings have white or light-colored roofs. They reflect more sunlight, so the building stays cooler inside on hot days. |
| 11 | Mirror writing | Some ambulances have their name printed backward on the front. In a car's rearview mirror, drivers see the word the right way around. |
| 13 | Lighthouse lenses | Many lighthouses use a special glass lens invented by Augustin-Jean Fresnel in the 1820s. It bends the lamp's light into a strong beam that ships can see from far away. |
| 15 | Periscopes | A periscope is a tube with a mirror at each end. Submarine crews use periscopes to see above the water while the submarine stays hidden below. |
