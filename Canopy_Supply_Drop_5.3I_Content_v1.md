# The Canopy Supply Drop — Content v1 (MA.5.3I-XP)

**Grade 5 · Math · Solara.** Written Sept 26, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/`.

**Main standard:** 5.3I · **Also shows up under:** 5.3J, 5.3L

**I can:** I can multiply a whole number by a fraction using pictures and area models. I can divide a unit fraction by a whole number, and a whole number by a unit fraction.

**Mission:** Multiply and divide with fractions to get supplies to every canopy camp.

**Story:** Solara's jungle camps live high in the rainforest canopy, and the rainy season is here. Every crate, tank, and trail has to be shared in fractions.

**Meters on Solara:** Camp spirit (starts at 30%) · Crates · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** Act 1 works with the task types already on the site. Acts 2–3 are written and graded but stay locked until the new task types are built.

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {name}, this is Commander Vega. The canopy camps on Solara need supplies before the heavy rains. Water, rope, and food all have to be split up fairly between the camps, and everything is measured in fractions. Help Kai and Nova get the supplies where they need to go. Vega out.
