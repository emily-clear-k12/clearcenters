# The Tide Pools — Content v1 (MA.3.3F-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Cloudreach.** Written Sept 26, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/`.

**Main standard:** 3.3F · **Also shows up under:** 3.3G, 3.3H

**Grade 3 limits:** Denominators 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 only. Every fraction is more than 0 and no more than 1.

**I can:** I can find fractions that are equal, using pictures and number lines. I can compare fractions that have the same top or bottom number.

**Mission:** Balance the tide pools and guide the sky whales to their nursery.

**Story:** The floating reefs of Cloudreach collect rain in tide pools, but a dry spell is coming. The crew has to balance the water so every sky creature has enough.

**Meters on Cloudreach:** Reef health (starts at 35%) · Pearls · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** Act 1 works with the task types already on the site. Acts 2–3 are written and graded but stay locked until the new task types are built.

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {name}, this is Commander Vega. The floating reefs of Cloudreach are drying out. The tide pools up there collect rainwater for the sky creatures, and each pool's gauge is marked a little differently. Help Kai and Nova match the pools so every creature has enough water. Vega out.
