## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mushrooms aren't plants | Mushrooms are not plants. They are fungi, and they can't make their own food. They get food by breaking down dead leaves and wood. |
| 2 | Chlorophyll | Chlorophyll is the green material inside leaves. It catches the Sun's light energy so the plant can make food. |
| 3 | Oxygen bubbles | Water plants give off tiny bubbles of oxygen when light shines on them. In dim light, the bubbles slow down or stop. |
| 5 | Greenhouse air | Some greenhouse farmers add extra carbon dioxide to the air inside. With more carbon dioxide, their plants can make more food and grow faster. |
| 6 | The willow tree test | About 400 years ago, a scientist grew a small willow tree in a pot of soil for 5 years. The tree gained about 74 kilograms, but the soil lost only a tiny bit. |
| 8 | Sugar on the move | Sugar made in the leaves travels through tubes in the stem to every part of the plant, even the roots underground. |
| 10 | Your breath feeds plants | Every time you breathe out, you give off carbon dioxide. Plants can use it to make food, and they give back oxygen for you to breathe. |
| 11 | Hidden green | Red and purple leaves still have green chlorophyll inside. Other colors cover up the green, but the leaves still use sunlight to make food. |
| 12 | Stretching for light | Plants grown in the dark grow tall, thin, and pale yellow. They stretch upward searching for light until their stored food runs out. |
| 15 | It starts with plants | Almost all food on Earth starts with producers. Bread comes from wheat, and even milk comes from cows that ate grass. |
