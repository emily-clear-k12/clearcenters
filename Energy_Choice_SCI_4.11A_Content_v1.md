# The Energy Choice — Content v1 (SCI.4.11A-XP)

**Grade 4 · Science · Mechara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/energy-choice.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.11A · **Also shows up under:** 4.11B, 4.11C, 4.1G, 4.2B, 4.3A, 4.3C, 4.4A, 4.5B

**I can:** I can identify renewable and nonrenewable resources. I can explain the advantages and disadvantages of using wind, water, sunlight, plants, animals, coal, oil, and natural gas.

**Mission:** Compare Earth's energy resources, weigh the good and the bad, and recommend a smart mix to power Gearfield.

**Story:** Mechara's city council is building a new district called Gearfield, and it needs power. The crew studies real Earth energy resources to help the council choose.

**Meters on Mechara:** District lit (starts at 25%) · Council votes · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. The robot city of Mechara just built a new district called Gearfield. The homes and streets are ready, but the city council hasn't chosen how to power it. They asked our crew for help. Energy works here just like it does on Earth. So we'll study Earth's real resources. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Where coal comes from | Coal formed from plants that grew in giant swamps long ago. The plants were buried and squeezed for millions of years until they turned into a black rock that burns. |
| 2 | Oil from the sea | Oil and natural gas formed from tiny sea plants and animals that died millions of years ago. They were buried under layers of mud and rock and slowly changed. |
| 3 | Crayon wax | Most crayons are made from paraffin wax. Paraffin wax is made from oil. |
| 5 | The Spindletop gusher | In 1901, workers drilling near Beaumont, Texas, hit oil. It shot more than 100 feet into the air and started the Texas oil boom. |
| 6 | Hoover Dam | Hoover Dam on the Colorado River uses falling water to spin machines that make electricity. It makes power for more than a million people in Nevada, Arizona, and California. |
| 7 | Texas wind | Texas makes more electricity from wind than any other state in the United States. Many wind farms stand on the windy plains of West Texas. |
| 8 | Smelly gas on purpose | Natural gas has no smell of its own. Gas companies add a chemical that smells like rotten eggs, so people can notice a leak right away. |
| 9 | Sunlight to spare | The sunlight that hits Earth in about an hour and a half carries as much energy as all the people on Earth use in a whole year. |
| 11 | Brush and save | Turning off the faucet while you brush your teeth can save up to 8 gallons of water a day. |
| 13 | Whale oil lamps | About 200 years ago, many lamps burned oil from whales. People hunted so many whales that some kinds almost disappeared. |
