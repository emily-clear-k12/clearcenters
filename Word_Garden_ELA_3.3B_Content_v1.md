# The Word Garden — Content v1 (ELA.3.3B-XP)

**Grade 3 · ELAR · Lumara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/word-garden.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.3B · **Also shows up under:** 3.3C, 3.3D

**I can:** I can use clues in and around a sentence to figure out new words and words with more than one meaning. I can use prefixes, suffixes, synonyms, antonyms, and idioms to understand words.

**Mission:** Read the gardener's guide, a story, and the garden newsletter. Use clues to decode tricky words, then send a word-perfect report to Mission Control.

**Story:** In the glowing garden on Lumara, words are as tricky as the plants. Bark isn't a dog sound, a bank holds no money, and a lost glow-seed needs finding.

**Meters on Lumara:** Glow bloom (starts at 10%) · Word seeds · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega in the Glow Garden on Lumara. The garden is full of words with more than one meaning, and Kai keeps mixing them up. When you meet a tricky word, don't skip it. Hunt for clues around it. Vega out.

## Passages

### Passage A · Gardener Pim's Guide to the Glow Garden (Guide (informational))

Welcome to the Glow Garden on Lumara! `A1.1` Every plant here is luminous. `A1.2` At night, the leaves give off a soft light, like tiny lamps. `A1.3` You can even read a book by their glow. `A1.4`

The glow-trees have silver bark. `A2.1` The bark is rough and thick, and it keeps the tree safe, the way your skin keeps you safe. `A2.2` Please do not peel it off. `A2.3`

The garden sits on the bank of the Shimmer Stream. `A3.1` The ground there is soft and muddy, so step with care. `A3.2` A spring bubbles up from under a big rock and fills the stream with fresh water. `A3.3`

To plant a glow-seed, dig a hole as deep as your thumb. `A4.1` Glow-seeds are light, so hold them tight, or the wind will carry them off. `A4.2` Water them every morning, and they will sprout in a few days. `A4.3`

### Passage B · The Lost Glow-Seed (Short story)

Juniper had one glow-seed left, and it was the brightest seed in the whole garden. `B1.1` She kept it in a tiny pouch tied with a blue bow. `B1.2` One windy morning, she opened the pouch, and the seed was gone! `B1.3`

Juniper felt dejected. `B2.1` Her shoulders drooped, and a tear rolled down her cheek. `B2.2` "I will never find it," she sighed. `B2.3`

Her friend Tam put a hand on her arm. `B3.1` "Keep your chin up," he said. "We will look together." `B3.2` They searched high and low, from the berry bushes to the stream. `B3.3`

At last, Tam spotted a faint twinkle under a leaf. `B4.1` It was not bright, but it was not dark, either. `B4.2` Juniper scooped up the seed and grinned from ear to ear. `B4.3` She planted it that very day, close to the spring. `B4.4`

### Passage C · The Glow Garden News (Newsletter)

This week, the glow-moths flew nonstop from sunset to sunrise. `C1.1` Their brightness lit up the leafy paths. `C1.2` By morning, they seemed to disappear, but they were only resting under the leaves. `C1.3`

Gardeners, please be careful near the muddy bank. `C2.1` Some glow-roots are almost invisible in the dark, and it is easy to trip on them. `C2.2` Presoak your glow-seeds in water for one hour before you plant them. `C2.3`

Next week, our helpers will import new soil into the garden in big carts. `C3.1` Rocks and old shells are nonliving, but they help the garden, too. `C3.2` Please do not dislike the glow-snails! They are helpful because they eat dead leaves. `C3.3`
