## How a 3- or 4-way idea fits this center

Each case has **two groups plus Neither**. That means ideas with three or four categories fit very well:

- **Three categories:** two become the groups, and the third becomes **Neither**. For wind / water / ice, the groups are Wind and Water, and Ice is Neither.
- **Four categories:** pick the two that students mix up most. Everything else goes in Neither.
- **The Venn uses a different pair of rules**, so the case gets a second look at the same idea.
- **A yes/no idea** (magnetic or not, even or odd) only works if something can honestly be "neither." Examples: a fraction is neither even nor odd, and 0 and 1 are neither prime nor composite. If nothing can be Neither, the idea isn't on this list.

## Things in Emily's list that shift a little

1. **Reflection / refraction / absorption / transmission.** 5.8C names only the first three: light is "reflected, refracted, or absorbed." So those three are the case: Reflected and Refracted are the groups, and Absorbed is Neither. Transmission can appear as an item, but it isn't a group, because the standard doesn't ask for it.
2. **Wind / water / ice is two standards.** 4.10B covers slow changes (weathering, erosion, deposition) caused by water, wind and ice. 5.10C covers landforms (deltas, canyons, sand dunes) made by wind, water and ice. Both are on the list, with different items.
3. **Learned behavior and inherited traits are two different standards, and mixing them up is itself a trap.** 4.13B is inherited vs. acquired *physical traits*. 5.13B is instinctual vs. learned *behaviors*. In the 4.13B case, a learned behavior is a Neither item: it isn't a physical trait at all.
4. **Observation vs. inference.** The science practice standards don't name inference, but ELAR 3.6F, 4.6F and 5.6F ("make inferences and use evidence") do. It's filed under ELAR, with science-flavored items.
5. **Correct / incorrect computation** conflicts with the spec in two ways. The spec says "do not write a math computation," and correct/incorrect is a yes/no, so page 2 can't have a real Neither. **Emily's call.** It may fit better in a fluency center. Not mapped.
6. **Page length.** Text structure, genre and word-problem items need two or three short sentences, which is more than the spec's Grade 3 cap of about 12 words per item. Each sentence would still stay short. The spec should say whether a 2–3 sentence item is allowed.
