# The Twin Legends — Content v1 (ELA.5.8A-XP)

**Grade 5 · ELAR · Lumara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/twin-legends.js`.

**Main standard:** 5.8A · **Also shows up under:** 5.9A, 5.6F, 5.7C, 5.8B, 5.7B, 5.3B

**I can:** I can infer more than one theme in a story, prove each theme with text evidence, and compare themes and genres across texts.

**Mission:** Infer the themes of two legends, prove each with evidence, find the theme they share, and tell legends apart from myths, fantasy, and science fiction.

**Story:** In Lumara's glowing Hollow Valley, two old legends are told at the lantern festival. They share one theme, but each one carries a theme of its own.

**Meters on Lumara:** Lantern light (starts at 15%) · Legend scrolls · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega in Lumara's Hollow Valley. Tonight is the lantern festival, and Elder Oren is telling the valley's oldest legends. Each legend carries more than one message. The lanterns on the Lantern Tree light up only when a theme is proven with evidence. Help us find every theme and light the valley. Vega out.

## Passages

### Passage A · The Twin Gardeners and the First Seed (Legend of Hollow Valley)

Long ago, when the first families came to Hollow Valley on Lumara, the ground was bare and gray. `A1.1` The elders say that two gardeners, the twins Aro and Ysa, were trusted with the valley's only glowseed. `A1.2` It was no bigger than a thumbnail, but it shone like a tiny star. `A1.3` "Whatever grows from this seed will feed the valley," the eldest gardener told them. `A1.4`

That autumn, the Long Dark came, a season when Lumara's sun did not rise for forty days. `A2.1` Aro wanted to hide the seed in a stone jar until the Long Dark ended. `A2.2` "If we plant it now, the cold might kill it," he said. `A2.3` Ysa shook her head. "If we wait, the valley will go hungry. A seed in a jar can never become a tree." `A2.4`

At last, Aro took a deep breath and knelt beside his sister. `A3.1` Together they planted the glowseed in the center of the valley. `A3.2` Then they began their vigil, and they did not leave the seedling's side for the whole Long Dark. `A3.3` While Ysa slept, Aro kept the frost away with warm stones, and while Aro slept, Ysa sang to the seedling so it would not be alone. `A3.4`

Alone, either twin would have fallen asleep in the snow. `A4.1` Taking turns, they lasted all forty nights. `A4.2` When the sun rose again, a silver tree stood where the seed had been, with glowing fruit on every branch. `A4.3` The Lantern Tree still stands in Hollow Valley, and the gardeners there still take turns watching over it. `A4.4`

### Passage B · Why the Moon-Lilies Glow (Legend of Hollow Valley)

In the days of the elders' grandparents, an old gardener named Neva lived at the edge of Hollow Valley. `B1.1` For fifty years, Neva shared everything her garden grew. `B1.2` She left baskets of berries on doorsteps, and she gave seeds to anyone who asked. `B1.3` In those days, the pale lilies beside her path did not glow at all. `B1.4`

When Neva grew very old, her eyes grew dim, and she could no longer find her way along the garden rows after sunset. `B2.1` One evening she stumbled on the path and spilled her whole basket of seeds. `B2.2` The next morning, her neighbors saw the seeds scattered in the dirt, and they understood. `B2.3`

No one could help Neva alone, so the whole village made a plan. `B3.1` Each family carried one bowl of moonwater from the spring and poured it on the lilies beside her path. `B3.2` The children took turns walking beside her every evening, and the baker built a railing along the rows. `B3.3`

On the ninth night, the lilies began to glow a soft silver, as if they had drunk the moonlight itself. `B4.1` Now Neva could see her path again, and she kept sharing her harvest until the end of her days. `B4.2` The elders say the lilies remembered her kindness and chose to return it. `B4.3` That is why, even now, the moon-lilies of Hollow Valley glow along every path at night. `B4.4`

### Passage C · Night Shift in Dome Seven (Story from the valley school)

In the year 3050, the gardens of Hollow Valley grow under clear domes that float above the fields. `C1.1` Dara works the night shift in Dome Seven with a helper robot named Pim. `C1.2`

Tonight, Dara's job is to test a new seed that is supposed to sprout in one hour instead of one week. `C2.1` She sets the growth lamp to blue light and checks the soil sensors on her wrist screen. `C2.2` Nothing happens, and the screen flashes: NO GROWTH DETECTED. `C2.3`

"This test is a failure," Dara groans, reaching to shut off the lamp. `C3.1` "Wait," beeps Pim. "Scientists learn from every test. Change one thing and try again." `C3.2` Dara lowers the lamp, adds more water, and waits. `C3.3`

At 3:00 a.m., a tiny green shoot pokes out of the soil. `C4.1` Dara grins and types a note for the morning crew: Test two worked. Test one taught me how. `C4.2`
