# The Outpost Debate — Content v1 (ELA.4.9E-XP)

**Grade 4 · ELAR · Frostveil.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/outpost-debate.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.9E · **Also shows up under:** 4.10A, 4.6F, 4.7C, 4.3B, 4.11D, 4.12C

**I can:** I can find the claim in an argument, explain how the author uses facts to support it, and tell who the argument was written for.

**Mission:** Read each argument, weigh the facts, figure out who each writer is talking to, and help the crew make the safest choice before the first storm.

**Story:** Winter is coming to Frostveil, and the crew can't agree. Kai wants to move camp to the valley. Nova wants to stay and strengthen the dome. Every argument is on the table.

**Meters on Frostveil:** Decision ready (starts at 15%) · Evidence cards · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. Winter is coming to Frostveil, and our crew has a big choice to make. Kai wants to move camp down to Pinecrest Valley. Nova wants to stay on the ridge and make our dome stronger. They both wrote arguments. Read them carefully. A strong argument needs more than a loud voice. Vega out.

## Passages

### Passage A · Kai's Proposal: Move to Pinecrest Valley (Argument (proposal))

Frostveil Outpost should move our camp down to Pinecrest Valley before winter! `A1.1` The wind up here on the ridge is fierce, and winter is coming fast. `A1.2`

Pinecrest Valley sits 300 meters lower than our ridge, so it is sheltered from the worst winds. `A2.1` Tall rock walls surround the valley on three sides. `A2.2` The valley also has a frozen lake that we could melt for drinking water. `A2.3`

Also, the valley is much prettier than this rocky ridge. `A3.1` I have a really good feeling about it! `A3.2` Moving would be a fun adventure for everyone! `A3.3`

The valley is the best place on all of Frostveil. `A4.1` Let's pack up and go! `A4.2`

### Passage B · Nova's Counter-Proposal: Stay and Strengthen the Dome (Argument (counter-proposal))

Our crew should stay on the ridge this winter and make our dome stronger. `B1.1` I know moving sounds exciting, but my measurements show it is not the safest choice. `B1.2`

For three weeks, I measured the wind and the new snow on the ridge and in the valley. `B2.1` The valley did have less wind, about half as much as the ridge. `B2.2` But the valley got more than twice as much new snow. `B2.3` Snow blows off the rock walls and piles up in deep drifts on the valley floor. `B2.4`

Our dome already has a heater, a landing pad, and a radio tower. `B3.1` Moving all of it would take about 12 days. `B3.2` The first big winter storm is expected in 9 days. `B3.3`

Kai is right that the wind is strong up here. `B4.1` We can block it by building a snow wall on the windy side and adding new bolts to the dome. `B4.2` Staying and strengthening our dome is the safest way to keep everyone warm this winter. `B4.3`

### Passage C · A Letter from Pilot Ruiz (Letter)

Dear Commander Vega, `C1.1`

I am writing about the crew's plan to move camp to Pinecrest Valley. `C2.1` As your supply pilot, I respectfully ask you to keep the outpost on the ridge this winter. `C2.2`

My supply plane needs a long, flat strip of ground to land safely. `C3.1` The landing pad on the ridge is 600 meters long and has bright landing lights. `C3.2` The flattest ground in the valley is only 250 meters long, with rock walls at both ends. `C3.3` Thick fog also fills the valley on most winter mornings. `C3.4`

If I cannot land, I cannot bring you food, fuel, or spare parts until spring. `C4.1` I know this decision is yours, and I trust your judgment. `C4.2` Please radio your answer before my next flight on Day 5. `C4.3`

Respectfully, `C5.1` Pilot Ansel Ruiz `C5.2`

### Passage D · Calling All Snow Pups! (Poster)

Calling all Snow Pups! `D1.1` Our dome is staying on the ridge, and YOU can help keep it warm and cozy! `D1.2`

Close the door fast! `D2.1` Every time the door stays open, warm air rushes out. `D2.2`

Wear your layers! `D3.1` Air gets trapped between your layers, and that trapped air helps keep you warm. `D3.2`

Fill a snow bag for the new snow wall! `D4.1` The wall will slow down the wind, like a giant shield for our dome. `D4.2`

Every Snow Pup who helps gets a gold star! `D5.1`
