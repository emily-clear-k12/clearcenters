# The Parts Depot — Content v1 (MA.3.4A-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Mechara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/parts-depot.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.4A · **Also shows up under:** 3.4B, 3.5A

**I can:** I can add and subtract within 1,000 using place value, number lines, and equations, and I can estimate to check if my answer makes sense.

**Mission:** Restock the Parts Depot, fill every robot's order, and keep the stock log exactly right.

**Story:** The robots of Mechara get their bolts, gears, springs, and wheels at the Parts Depot. The crew has to add deliveries, subtract orders, and keep the stock log right.

**Meters on Mechara:** Depot stocked (starts at 20%) · Shelves filled · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. We've landed in the robot city on Mechara. Every robot here gets its bolts, gears, springs, and wheels from one place: the Parts Depot. Help Kai and Nova add the deliveries, subtract the orders, and keep the shelves full. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Six-sided bolts | Most bolt heads have six flat sides. Six sides give a wrench lots of places to grip, even in a tight spot. |
| 3 | Rust | Iron and steel can rust when they stay wet in the air. Rust is flaky and reddish-brown, and it makes the metal weaker. |
| 5 | Rivets | The Eiffel Tower in France is held together by about 2.5 million metal pins called rivets. |
| 6 | Steel | Steel is made mostly of iron with a tiny bit of carbon mixed in. That makes it much stronger than plain iron. |
| 8 | Righty-tighty | Most bolts and screws tighten when you turn them to the right, the way a clock's hands go. People say "righty-tighty, lefty-loosey." |
| 10 | The first wheels | Some of the very first wheels were not used for carts. People used spinning wheels to shape clay pots. |
| 11 | Stainless steel | Stainless steel has a metal called chromium mixed in. The chromium helps keep it from rusting. |
| 13 | Bike chains | On a bike, a chain links the gear by the pedals to a gear on the back wheel. Pedaling pulls the chain and turns the wheel. |
| 14 | The first Ferris wheel | The first Ferris wheel was built in Chicago in 1893. It was more than 250 feet tall. |
| 15 | Aluminum cans | An aluminum can can be recycled and made into a new can again and again. |
