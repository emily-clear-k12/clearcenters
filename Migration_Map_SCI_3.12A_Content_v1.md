# The Migration Map — Content v1 (SCI.3.12A-XP)

**Grade 3 · Science · Cloudreach.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/migration-map.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.12A · **Also shows up under:** 3.1E, 3.2B, 3.2C, 3.3A, 3.3C, 3.5B, 3.5G

**I can:** I can explain how temperature and rain change what animals do and how they grow. I can tell about migration, hibernation, and how plants go dormant.

**Mission:** Track real Earth animals as the seasons change, and explain how weather makes them migrate, hibernate, or go dormant.

**Story:** From the sky reef of Cloudreach, the crew can watch animals all over Earth. As winter comes, the animals start to move, rest, or hang on. The crew is building a map of it all.

**Meters on Cloudreach:** Flyway map (starts at 10%) · Field tags · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. From the sky reef, our scope can see animals all over Earth. Winter is coming there. Some animals are taking long trips. Some are settling in for a long rest. Help us figure out what each animal does and why the weather makes it happen. Let's build the Migration Map. Vega out.

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Gray whale road trip | Gray whales eat in cold Arctic waters in summer. In winter they swim to warm lagoons in Mexico to have their calves. The round trip can be 10,000 miles or more. |
| 3 | Fueling up | Before migrating, a ruby-throated hummingbird eats so much that it can almost double its weight. Many fly nonstop about 500 miles across the Gulf of Mexico. |
| 5 | Months without a meal | A hibernating black bear can go months without eating, drinking, or going to the bathroom. It lives off the fat it stored in the fall. |
| 6 | Puffball birds | Chickadees stay all winter in places that freeze. They fluff up their feathers to trap warm air, like wearing a puffy coat. |
| 8 | The biggest bat colony | Bracken Cave near San Antonio, Texas, is the summer home of millions of Mexican free-tailed bats. It is the biggest bat colony in the world. |
| 9 | Buried turtles | In winter, box turtles dig down into soft soil or leaves and stay still for months. Their bodies slow way down until warm weather returns. |
| 10 | The tallest bird | Whooping cranes are the tallest birds in North America, about 5 feet tall. The only wild flock that migrates on its own flies from Canada to the Texas coast every fall. |
| 12 | Long-distance walkers | Some caribou herds in Alaska and Canada travel more than 1,000 miles in a year. That's one of the longest trips of any land animal. |
| 13 | Slow bat hearts | An active little brown bat's heart beats hundreds of times a minute. While it hibernates, its heart can slow to as few as 10 beats a minute. |
| 15 | Loud winter guests | Many sandhill cranes spend the winter in Texas. Their loud, rattling calls can be heard more than a mile away. |
