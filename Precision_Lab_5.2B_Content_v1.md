# The Precision Lab — Content v1 (MA.5.2B-XP)

**Grade 5 · Math · Frostveil.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/precision-lab.js`.

**Main standard:** 5.2B · **Also shows up under:** 5.2A, 5.2C

**I can:** I can compare and order decimals to the thousandths using >, <, and =, show digit values in expanded form, and round decimals to tenths or hundredths.

**Mission:** Measure, compare, and order the lab's ice samples to the thousandth so the prize cores ship out on time.

**Story:** The Precision Lab on Frostveil measures ice cores, crystals, and snowmelt to the thousandth. The crew needs help comparing, ordering, and rounding every reading.

**Meters on Frostveil:** Lab precision (starts at 15%) · Core tubes · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| 1 | Tiny millimeters | A millimeter is one thousandth of a meter. So 0.001 meter is 1 millimeter, about as thick as a paper clip wire. |

| 6 | Air from long ago | Ice cores have tiny air bubbles trapped inside. Scientists study that old air to learn what Earth's air was like long ago. |
| 7 | Salt leaves sea ice | Sea ice that lasts through a summer loses most of its salt. Arctic explorers have melted old sea ice for drinking water. |
| 8 | Diamond dust | On very cold, clear days, tiny ice crystals can float in the air and sparkle in the sun. This is called diamond dust. |
| 9 | Lakes freeze on top | Lakes freeze from the top down. The ice works like a lid, and fish keep swimming in the liquid water underneath. |
