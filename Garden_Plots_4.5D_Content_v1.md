# The Garden Plots — Content v1 (MA.4.5D-XP)

**Grade 4 · Math · Lumara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/garden-plots.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.5D · **Also shows up under:** 4.5C

**I can:** I can use the formulas P = 2l + 2w, P = 4s, and A = l × w to solve problems about the perimeter and area of rectangles, including finding a missing side.
