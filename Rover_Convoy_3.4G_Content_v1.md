# The Rover Convoy — Content v1 (MA.3.4G-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Frostveil.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/rover-convoy.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.4G · **Also shows up under:** 3.4F, 3.4I

**I can:** I can multiply a two-digit number by a one-digit number using partial products, properties, and the standard algorithm. I can tell if a product is even or odd.

**Mission:** Multiply to load every rover in the convoy and get it across the ice.

**Story:** The rover convoy is about to cross the great ice field of Frostveil. Every rover needs fuel cells, heat packs, and chains, and the crew has to multiply fast to load them all.

**Meters on Frostveil:** Convoy loaded (starts at 30%) · Rovers ready · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega. We're at the motor pool on the edge of Frostveil's great ice field. Our rover convoy has to cross the ice to reach the far outpost. Each rover needs the same load, so we'll be multiplying a lot. Help Kai and Nova load every rover before the storm window closes. Vega out.
