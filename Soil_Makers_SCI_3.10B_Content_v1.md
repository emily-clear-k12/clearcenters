# The Soil Makers — Content v1 (SCI.3.10B-XP)

**Grade 3 · Science · Cindara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/soil-makers.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.10B · **Also shows up under:** 3.10C, 3.1B, 3.1E, 3.2B, 3.2C, 3.2D, 3.3A, 3.3C, 3.5B, 3.5G

**I can:** I can explain how soils like sand and clay form from weathered rock and from dead plants and animals that decompose. I can compare soils by grain size, feel, and how much water they hold, and tell fast changes from slow ones.

**Mission:** Find out how soil forms, test sand, clay, and potting soil, and choose the best soil for the camp garden.

**Story:** Cindara's old lava rock is slowly turning into soil. To plant the first garden, the crew studies how real Earth soil forms and which soil is best for growing food.

**Meters on Cindara:** Garden grown (starts at 15%) · Soil cores · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

## Discoveries (Earth-true)

| Task | Discovery | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Quartz sand | A lot of sand is made of quartz. Quartz is a very hard mineral, so its grains last a long time after the rest of the rock has worn away. |
| 5 | Rotting logs | A big fallen tree can take many years, even decades, to rot away. Fungi, bacteria, and insects break it down little by little. |
| 6 | Slow soil | It can take hundreds of years for nature to make just 2.5 centimeters (1 inch) of new topsoil. That's why people work hard to protect the soil we have. |
| 7 | Clay pots | People have made pots from clay for more than 10,000 years. Wet clay can be shaped, and it turns hard when it is baked very hot. |
| 8 | Pill bugs | Pill bugs, also called roly-polies, are not insects. They are related to crabs and shrimp, and they eat dead leaves and wood. |
| 9 | Tiny earthquakes | Earth has many earthquakes every day, but most are so small that people can't feel them. |
| 10 | Grain sizes | Clay grains are the tiniest soil grains. A big sand grain can be more than 1,000 times wider than a clay grain. |
| 11 | Lichens | Lichens can grow on bare rock. They slowly help break the rock apart. When they die, they add bits of living remains that help start new soil. |
| 13 | The Texas state soil | Texas has a state soil called Houston Black. It is a dark clay soil found in the Blackland Prairie, and it is great for growing crops. |
| 15 | Loam | Gardeners call soil with a good mix of sand, silt, and clay loam. Loam holds some water but still lets extra water drain away. |
