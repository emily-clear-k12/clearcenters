# The Seed Crates — Content v1 (MA.3.4K-XP)

**Grade 3 · Math · Lumara.** Written Sept 27, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/seed-crates.js`.

**Main standard:** 3.4K · **Also shows up under:** 3.4D, 3.4E, 3.4H, 3.4J, 3.5B

**I can:** I can solve one-step and two-step multiplication and division problems using arrays, equal groups, number lines, and facts I know.

**Mission:** Pack the seed crates, plant the glow rows, and get the garden blooming for moon-bloom night.

**Story:** Planting season has come to the glowing garden on Lumara. The crew has to pack seed crates in equal groups and plant them in rows before moon-bloom night.

**Meters on Lumara:** Garden glow (starts at 20%) · Seed crates · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).
