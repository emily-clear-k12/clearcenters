# The Crew Poems — Content v1 (ELA.4.9B-XP)

**Grade 4 · ELAR · Lumara.** Written Sept 28, 2026. Code: `lib/cases/expedition-station/quests/crew-poems.js`.

**Main standard:** 4.9B · **Also shows up under:** 4.10D, 4.3B, 4.6F, 4.12A, 4.11D

**I can:** I can find similes, metaphors, and personification in a poem and explain the picture they create. I can tell lines, stanzas, and rhymes apart.

**Mission:** Explain the similes, metaphors, and personification in the crew's poems, then add a poem of your own to the Poem Wall.

**Story:** On Lumara, the crew holds a poetry night in the Glow Garden. Nova, Kai, Vega, and a Lumaran gardener each read a poem full of glowing bugs, yawning trees, and moons that look like lanterns.

**Meters on Lumara:** Stage lights (starts at 25%) · Poem cards · Journal (10 discoveries)

**Status:** All 3 acts are playable. Every task is graded (written answers are scored by the teacher).

**Opening (Vega):** Specialist {firstName}, this is Commander Vega in the Glow Garden on Lumara. Tonight the Lumarans invited our crew to poetry night. Each of us wrote a poem, and so did Gardener Oreli. The Lumarans say a poem isn't finished until someone explains the pictures it paints. Help us do that. Vega out.

## Passages

### Passage A · The Lantern Bugs (Poem by Nova (rhyming))

The lantern bugs come out at night, `A1.1` like sparks that jumped from a campfire bright. `A1.2` They float above the flower beds, `A1.3` like tiny moons on silver threads. `A1.4`

Their wings hum soft as a whispered song, `A2.1` and the garden listens all night long. `A2.2` When morning comes, they dim and fade, `A2.3` and sleep beneath the leafy shade. `A2.4`

### Passage B · Night Shift (Poem by Kai (free verse))

The glow garden is a busy city at night. `B1.1` The fern-lamps are streetlights. `B1.2` Beetles are buses, humming down the stem roads. `B1.3`

Up high, the moon is a silver lantern `B2.1` hanging over the leafy rooftops. `B2.2` And me? `B2.3` I am the night guard, counting every light. `B2.4`

### Passage C · Evening Watch (Poem by Commander Vega)

At dusk, the old glowtree stretches her arms. `C1.1` She yawns, and her leaves wake up, one by one. `C1.2` The breeze whispers the news of the day to her. `C1.3`

The flowers nod good night to the setting sun. `C2.1` The path glows green beneath my boots. `C2.2` I walk home slowly with my hands in my pockets. `C2.3`

### Passage D · Harvest Song (Poem by Gardener Oreli of Lumara)

My garden is a quilt of light, `D1.1` stitched with rows of blue and gold. `D1.2` The sweet smell of moonmelons drifts over the fence. `D1.3`

The honey-moths are busy as bakers, `D2.1` dusting every flower with yellow pollen. `D2.2` Crunchy leaves snap and crackle under my feet. `D2.3`

Tomorrow we will pick and share, `D3.1` and the garden will sing its thank-you song. `D3.2`

### Passage E · Kai's card for the Poem Wall (Note (has mistakes))

Our crew came to Lumara for **are**`[E:are]` first poetry night. Nova's lantern bugs **glows**`[E:glows]` like sparks. The old glowtree **stretch**`[E:stretch]` her arms at dusk. **i**`[E:i]` will never forget this garden.
